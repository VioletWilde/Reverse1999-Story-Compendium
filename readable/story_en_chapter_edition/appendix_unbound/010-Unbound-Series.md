## 801001 · Script 801001

Laplace's unpredictable inventor is busy building another mysterious device on the island.

It appears to resemble a catapult.

**X**: Hey, Vertin, what a lucky surprise.

**X**: I'm setting up a new Goldberg machine, this place is going to be a hub.

**X**: When a seagull glides over the ocean and builds a nest on one end of this lever, a stone will be flung from the other end.

**X**: Which will fly all the way to ...

**X**: Hm. Though the fun will be spoiled if I tell you now. See for yourself when I'm done.

**X**: Why don't we talk about something else, like Laplace? You might wanna hear what a mess it is over there.

- **Choice:** Has Laplace decided who will be Lucy's successor?

- **Choice:** I heard an Awakened employee started a hunger strike

- **Choice:** I'd better get going

**X**: They have no idea who can take her place. She was much loved, as one could tell from that epic farewell.

**X**: Nobody has the courage to sit on her recharger throne, as you never know when you'll get burned.

**X**: And it will take some time to transform the office back into a space for living beings to use.

**X**: I have to admit that sometimes I prefer the chaos. Since nobody has time to tell us what to do, we already have exciting projects in the pipeline.

- **Choice:** I'd better get going

**X**: I'll catch you later, I've got a couple of gadgets to tune up.

**X**: You mean Ulrich. He's still on hunger strike, camping out in front of the committee building day in and day out.

**X**: He was alone at first, then some human researchers and arcanists joined him.

**X**: Even though none of them could last as long as Ulrich.

**X**: Eventually the Foundation employees arrived, pushing a cart full of food. Ulrich's scream of "traitors!" was so loud that it was hard to believe he could make such a sound.

**X**: But he also has a lot of work to do, so I assume he'll be there a few days and will have to end the strike as deadlines approach.

- **Choice:** I'd better get going
