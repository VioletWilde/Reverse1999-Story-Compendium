## 808601 · Script 808601

A lady sits in front of the IDM, her expression emotionless. She places a hand on her face, her fingers carefully running over her cheeks as if they were unfamiliar.

For a brief moment, you can't quite place who she is. You recognize the metal torso and limbs, but her face seems unfamiliar.

**Lucy**: Nice to meet you, Timekeeper.

**Lucy**: I have intended to visit you, but training interfered. Forgive me for meeting you like this instead.

**Vertin**: Training?

**Lucy**: Yes, training. I was instructed to leave Laplace and go on a vacation.

**Lucy**: Just as I was wondering where and how I was going to accomplish this task, Madam Z recommended your suitcase.

**Lucy**: So I prepared this spare body for easier communication with the members in your suitcase.

**Lucy**: Considering the presence of underage individuals, I opted for a softer material. Hopefully they will be comfortable with the temperature of my metal.

**Lucy**: As for my training, it is to master the art of resting.

**Lucy**: I would like to inquire, how does one define the concept of resting?

- **Choice:** Resting is when you stop working for a while

- **Choice:** Resting is when you focus on keeping yourself in good shape

- **Choice:** I'll catch up with you later; we can discuss it then

**Lucy**: I am glad that you and I have come to the same conclusion.

**Lucy**: Based on my observations, resting is what one does to recharge their physical and mental systems.

**Lucy**: Through the combination of a balanced diet, suitable exercise, and sufficient sleep.

**Lucy**: But these measures do not work for the Awakened.

**Lucy**: I require no sleep. My sustenance is electricity, and exercise quickens my deterioration.

**Lucy**: I suppose the closest definition of resting is the continuous charging of electricity.

**Lucy**: But from your perspective, can this not be categorized as the sin of gluttony?

- **Choice:** I'll catch up with you later; we can discuss it then

**Lucy**: See you soon, Timekeeper. Remember, we still need to complete the resting plan.

**Lucy**: Apologies for the interruption, Timekeeper. This is a paradox.

**Lucy**: My work has ordered me to rest. If I were to stop working,

**Lucy**: then I would have to stop resting too. Please provide an alternative suggestion.

- **Choice:** I'll catch up with you later; we can discuss it then
