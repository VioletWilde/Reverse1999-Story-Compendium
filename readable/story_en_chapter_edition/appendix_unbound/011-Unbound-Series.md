## 851201 · Script 851201

Your direct supervisor chooses the perfect place to meet. It's not too eye-catching, nor is it so quiet that you can't find her easily.

She's holding a medal in one hand and an award announcement speech in the other. Her thermos cup is left aside.

**Z**: Congratulations, Vertin. The Foundation is pleased with your recent performance.

**Z**: I am here to award you on behalf of the Foundation.

She puts the medal and speech in a folder and hands them to you.

**Z**: I know you are not a fan of these ceremonies. So here is your medal, and a brief statement on your achievements.

- **Choice:** Are people still celebrating at the Foundation?

- **Choice:** I can't express how much I appreciate your help, Madam Z

- **Choice:** Is there a new mission?

**Z**: You could say that.

**Z**: But some wasted no time in securing their next step.

**Z**: They are already seeking partnership with Laplace on the development of the Equilibrium Umbrella.

**Z**: The umbrella will allow the Foundation to move freely during the "Storm." The committee should announce a new bill on field investigations soon.

**Z**: The balance within the Foundation will shift once more. The politicians will be busy.

**Z**: Speaking of balance, we should be equipping frontline investigators with Equilibrium Umbrellas as a top priority.

- **Choice:** Is there a new mission?

**Z**: No need to rush, Vertin. Take your time and rest.

**Z**: Enjoy the company of your friends before you set out again.

**Z**: Don't worry about the reports, I'll handle them. You've been busy ever since becoming the Timekeeper, and you deserve a break.

**Z**: That was nothing compared to what you and your friends have done.

**Z**: Even though we now have the Equilibrium Umbrella to shield us from the "Storm,"

**Z**: we still have ways to go before we can truly understand it.

**Z**: We will get there, Vertin, slowly but surely.

- **Choice:** Is there a new mission?
