## 804101 · Script 804101

**Matilda**: Vertin!

The voice's owner has found you before you could even look for her.

Like a trained athlete, the divination genius charges towards you, her orbuculum secured under her arm.

**Matilda**: Aha! Finally caught you!

**Matilda**: Think you can hide from me? Did you really think you could sneak off to the twist ball without the knowledge of the monitor assistant Matilda?

**Vertin**: Twist ball?

**Matilda**: You can't fool me. The lovely Sotheby has given away the details of your plan.

**Matilda**: You should feel lucky that I'm soft-hearted and generous with other people's mistakes. Now, if you invite me to this ball, I will forget about what happened.

**Vertin**: There is no twist ball, Matilda.

**Matilda**: Do you really expect me to believe that?! I ...

She sees the genuine look on your face, and her confidence starts to waver.

**Matilda**: Ah, do you mean that? Was Ms. Sotheby pulling a prank on me?

**Vertin**: Perhaps she forgot that she was supposed to hold the ball.

**Matilda**: So no one was left out, and she won't be doing some silly twist dance.

**Matilda**: Hmph! You'd better be telling the truth. I hope you will come up with a more tasteful event next time.

**Matilda**: And be sure to invite me! As the monitor assistant, I am obligated to be there and supervise.

- **Choice:** You're welcome to join us

- **Choice:** There could be a party if you'd like

- **Choice:** Would you mind excusing me? I'll catch up with you soon

**Matilda**: Join, join you? You mean leaving here with you, also with, with, with her?

**Matilda**: *sneering* Hmph! You can't trick me into breaking the rules. I will become a Foundation investigator all on my own.

**Matilda**: And I will outdo you and Sonetto in this position!

**Matilda**: By then, you will be hoping to join me, not the reverse!

- **Choice:** Would you mind excusing me? I'll catch up with you soon

**Matilda**: Which is ...? Not another ball without me, I should hope.

**Matilda**: You're cleverer than this, Vertin! You know you can't fool me. I will be watching.

**Matilda**: I, I never said I wanted a party!

**Matilda**: Unlike you, I've been keeping myself occupied as the monitor assistant.

**Matilda**: And I already have plans for my next holiday, and I will be visiting my mother in the one after that.

**Matilda**: If you want me at your party, make sure it is scheduled after my family time.

- **Choice:** Would you mind excusing me? I'll catch up with you soon
