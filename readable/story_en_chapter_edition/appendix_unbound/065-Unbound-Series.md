## 800006 · Somewhere in the Suitcase

> **Title Card:** Somewhere in the Suitcase

**Sotheby**: Ms. Bouanich's vision says the float has to be decorated with only "the most simple and ordinary things."

**Sotheby**: But royal crowns are usually made of gold ... unless this is some kind of riddle?

**Sonetto**: Ms. Sotheby, if we actually create a pure gold crown for our La Papesse des folles, that is, the Timekeeper's float ...

**Lilya**: Then we'll have a Vertin float crushed into a blinchik, haha!

**Sonetto**: In Carnival tradition, La Papesse des folles stands for a world turned upside down—rich and poor, noble and common, sacred and secular, all flipped around.

**Sonetto**: So, then we might be better off searching for materials that are the direct opposite of the conventional.

**Sotheby**: Opposite ... Look! What's that over there?

A line of Critters walks unsteadily by, balancing large pieces of flattened cardboard boxes on their heads.

Without attracting any attention, the chief assistant collects a piece of cardboard.

**Sonetto**: This could be exactly what we've been looking for.

**Lilya**: Who was the last person that tried taking materials from these little guys? Was it Medicine Pocket?

**Sotheby**: I do believe these creatures would happily trade with us if we can provide them with a sufficient amount of Dust.

**Sotheby**: Our materials are secured, so the next step must be writing the coronation blessings on the crown.

**Sotheby**: But I haven't any idea what to say. Maybe you can find the right words, Sonetto.

The chief assistant pulls out her glasfeder. Delicately twirling it over the cardboard as she thinks. Finally, she launches into a dance of delicate strokes that begin to glow faintly as she raises her glasfeder.

**Sonetto**: I believe in you.
