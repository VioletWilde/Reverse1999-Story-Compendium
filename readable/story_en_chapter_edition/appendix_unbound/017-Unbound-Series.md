## 800301 · Script 800301

The druid is taking a stroll through the war-torn woods.

She rests her hand on the scarred trunk of an olive tree, intently listening to its quiet whispers in the wind.

**Druvis III**: A beautiful morning to you, Vertin.

**Druvis III**: As you described, this is a forest worth looking forward to.

**Druvis III**: The branches of the trees are arranged in a perfect pattern, showing a sense of harmony and unity. I can see the gentle and caring nature of the guardian of the forest.

**Vertin**: That is a high praise.

**Druvis III**: Indeed. All branches follow the same pattern and grow in the same way.

**Druvis III**: The edges of squares give rise to triangles, and the edges of triangles give rise to squares.

**Druvis III**: I may not be familiar with this trimming style, but I can hear the trees humming with joy.

**Vertin**: That pattern is called the Pythagoras tree.

**Druvis III**: I have heard of this Pythagoras. He was a mathematician.

**Druvis III**: Little did I know that his theorem could be expressed on trees.

**Druvis III**: My respects to the sagacity of the ancients.

- **Choice:** How have you been keeping?

- **Choice:** Arcana has finally been defeated

- **Choice:** I should get going

**Druvis III**: I'm grateful for your concern, Vertin.

**Druvis III**: I am well. The Foundation did not make it hard for me to repair the square.

**Druvis III**: Instead, they collected data on the potential damage that the proliferating plants could cause to the Foundation's infrastructure.

**Druvis III**: They also thoroughly examined the growth potions made by Ms. Sotheby.

**Druvis III**: So it seems our next protest will need a new strategy, it won't be as easy.

- **Choice:** I should get going

**Druvis III**: I would love to talk to the trees again, they are delightful. Farewell for now.

**Druvis III**: Defeating Arcana ... I am not certain she can be defeated.

**Druvis III**: To defeat an entity like her, one must first understand her intentions.

**Druvis III**: Manus Vindictae's purpose is clear, but Arcana's is a mystery.

**Druvis III**: She didn't care about anything, based on the time I spent with her.

**Druvis III**: In accordance with the laws of nature, fruits fall, seeds take root, and trees bear new produce.

**Druvis III**: The tree can be pruned, its fruit thinned, and even cut down to change its appearance.

**Druvis III**: Yet, the inherent law of nature cannot be altered.

**Druvis III**: To me, Arcana is like an unchangeable law, one without any purpose. It cannot be overcome, just as it cannot be changed.

- **Choice:** I should get going
