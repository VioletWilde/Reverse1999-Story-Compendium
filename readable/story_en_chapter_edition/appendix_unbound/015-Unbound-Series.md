## 802701 · Script 802701

The green radio is basking under the sun, bathing in its warmth.

Since she doesn't have a human face to make expressions, it's hard to read her mood.

However, since the 5th Dimension's "Aquarius" is playing from her speakers, it might suggest she is in good spirits.

She snaps up the moment she notices your presence, and the music shifts to a four-note motif—just like Beethoven's fate knocking at the door.

**Ms. Radio**: He-hello? Ms. Vertin, what brings you here? Have they finally decided my verdict?

**Ms. Radio**: I hope my recent actions were enough to prove my innocence ...

She shivers nervously, and the music seems to crackle along with her.

- **Choice:** Of course In fact,<br>we couldn't have achieved any of this without you

- **Choice:** Could you find a person for me?

- **Choice:** I shall leave you to your leisure

**Ms. Radio**: Really? That is wonderful news! I am relieved to hear that I have been helpful.

**Ms. Radio**: These past few days have left me on edge, wondering if they would pry open my battery cover, or submerge me in Formalin ...

**Ms. Radio**: ... or even take me apart in search of enemy signals ...

**Ms. Radio**: Thank you for your confidence in me, Ms. Vertin.

**Ms. Radio**: So, am I allowed to move freely within the Foundation now?

**Ms. Radio**: I could use a little cleaning, polishing, and perhaps a coat of wax, if I can find someone to do it.

- **Choice:** I shall leave you to your leisure

**Ms. Radio**: In that case, why don't we enjoy a little pampering time together?

**Ms. Radio**: We could slather ourselves in machine oil, bask in the sun, and let the rays zap our impurities away.

**Ms. Radio**: Certainly. Which DJ do you have in mind? Or are we looking for updates on a particular politician?

**Ms. Radio**: From AM to FM, I'll scan through all the frequencies, just for you.

**Vertin**: The name is Urd.

**Ms. Radio**: Ah, the biographer. She is the reason we embarked on this great journey. What intrigues you about her?

**Vertin**: ... I can't be sure. It's just a gut feeling.

**Ms. Radio**: Oh ... I didn't mean to pry.

**Ms. Radio**: I will gladly help and pay close attention to the name. If it comes up in the news, I'll let you know right away.

- **Choice:** I shall leave you to your leisure
