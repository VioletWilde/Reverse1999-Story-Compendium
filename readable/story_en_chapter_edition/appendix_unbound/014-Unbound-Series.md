## 805701 · Script 805701

The dream-builder stands perfectly still by the sea, unaware of her surroundings.

A stack of gilded white umbrellas lies piled at her feet.

She keeps her weary eyes shut, her body swaying gently in the sea breeze.

- **Choice:** Shake her awake

- **Choice:** Put a jacket on her

**Mesmer Jr.**: Stop it! Bring me more straps, I need to hold him down ...

She gestures with her hands, still in her dreams as if she's stifling a scream.

Her actions throw off her balance and wake her up. She opens her eyes.

**Mesmer Jr.**: Vertin?

**Mesmer Jr.**: *sigh* ... Thought I would get some rest.

**Mesmer Jr.**: It's been a while since we saw each other outside the Foundation, hasn't it?

**Mesmer Jr.**: I should probably thank you for the extra work you're causing me. Finally, I have a chance to escape the daily grind and enjoy some fresh air.

**Mesmer Jr.**: Even though some of the Foundation staff are starting to see me as your go-between ...

**Mesmer Jr.**: Whatever. It's nice to get away from the screaming patients once in a while. Here, the Equilibrium Umbrellas you requested.

- **Choice:** Have you ever considered a different job?

- **Choice:** How have the Laplace researchers been doing since the curse?

- **Choice:** Thank you for bringing the umbrellas

**Mesmer Jr.**: To do what? Be your errand girl?

**Mesmer Jr.**: Sadly, the number of people losing their minds only increased over the years. I'll never be able to leave this job.

**Mesmer Jr.**: You should continue your efforts, Vertin. Find ways to minimize the impact of the "Storm" on people, as you did with the Equilibrium Umbrella.

**Mesmer Jr.**: That'll save me a lot of trouble.

- **Choice:** Thank you for bringing the umbrellas

**Mesmer Jr.**: It's no big deal. Just part of the job.

**Mesmer Jr.**: Now, if you don't mind, please verify the delivery and confirm receipt of the items you requested.

**Mesmer Jr.**: The faster you count, the sooner ... I can ... take a break.

Her heavy eyelids slide closed again as she drifts into another dream.

**Mesmer Jr.**: They are managing, but unfortunately, some were beyond saving when they recited the incantation.

**Mesmer Jr.**: The survivors were impacted in two different ways. Some looked fine on the outside, but their minds were definitely unstable.

**Mesmer Jr.**: Of those people, some were just tired and having a bad day. I put them to sleep. That was easy.

**Mesmer Jr.**: There were also people who were severely deformed by the curse. They had changed so much that they were unrecognizable.

**Mesmer Jr.**: These patients quickly adapted to their new appearance and began to enjoy scaring their colleagues.

**Mesmer Jr.**: Sometimes their pranks would go too far, and the traumatized victims would end up coming to me.

**Mesmer Jr.**: The sane arcanists are now more troublesome than the insane.

**Mesmer Jr.**: It just goes to show how unpredictable arcanists can be.

- **Choice:** Thank you for bringing the umbrellas

**Mesmer Jr.**: Urgh ... It's hot.

**Mesmer Jr.**: Please move these people out into the hallway, this room's turning into a sauna.

Shimmering sweat builds on her forehead until she slowly begins opening her eyes.

**Mesmer Jr.**: Vertin?

**Mesmer Jr.**: Thanks for the jacket. But I doubt anyone will catch a cold in this weather.
