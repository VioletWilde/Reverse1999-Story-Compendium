## 800001 · Script 800001

**Lucy**: I am glad that we have once again braved the "Storm" together. Here is a special gift from the LSCC. It is the result of many intense discussions.

**Lucy**: You may rest assured, Timekeeper, that "Dalpo No.1" has passed all safety measures. We also erased the angry roaring sounds that our staff recorded during internal testing.

**Sonetto**: Hello, Timekeeper. This feels a little strange. It's like ... like I'm calling you on the phone.

**Sonetto**: I know we still have a long way to go before we put an end to the "Storm" ... but this is still a milestone that's worth celebrating, don't you think? I'll never forget this day. I hope you can enjoy the celebration, too!

**Regulus**: Aha! Finally, we've got an anniversary of our own! You're lucky it's a cool one. Otherwise, I'd have had to make one up on the spot! Maybe ... the anniversary of APPLe I's death!

**Regulus**: To be honest, I've always thought the suitcase could use some sprucing up with something classy, and now, look what we've got—a phonograph with a big silver ear! It doesn't get more classy than that! I bet this baby would rock my Johnny Cash records.

**APPLe**: My friends, you must feel weary after an entire year of adventure. This APPLe believes that we could use a small break to relax our nerves and organize our thoughts.

**APPLe**: I do hope this video doesn't capture how drunk this APPLe is. If you hear any groundless conjectures or inappropriate complaints from me, please don't take them seriously.

**Lilya**: Hello, Vertin? Hm. This thing sounds much clearer than the old "anti-interference" communication devices they still use in Zeno ... It could be a little smaller, though.

**Lilya**: Oh, remember to ask Bunny Bunny to buy some vodka and juice when she gets groceries. You know, I'm better at clinking glasses than giving speeches.
