## 800401 · Script 800401

That 768-nautical-mile flight was barely a warmup for the pilot and her trusty broom.

Perched at the highest point on the island, she takes a moment to fully savor her most recent flight.

She takes a deep swig from her canteen before shaking her head.

**Lilya**: Hey, Vertin! Come, drink with me.

**Lilya**: Only kids like Regulus drink soda. You should get used to Vodka.

**Vertin**: I'm underage too.

**Lilya**: Sheesh. You were never the model student, but now you sound like one.

**Lilya**: If you're not into drinking holy water, at least chat with me.

**Lilya**: Or the joy of drinking will be lost.

- **Choice:** Why did you decide to become a pilot?

- **Choice:** Have you heard anything from Zeno?

- **Choice:** I should get going

**Lilya**: Now that's a topic to get the sips flowing! Who isn't up for a little reminiscing while drinking?

**Lilya**: I became a pilot because I love flying, obviously!

**Lilya**: Ah, the wind rushing past you, the thrill of acceleration ...

**Lilya**: ... the weightlessness and adrenaline while chasing the enemy.

**Lilya**: The best part is the screaming when you slice apart their plane.

**Lilya**: Call me disloyal if you want, but to me, Zeno was just a place to get a pilot's license.

**Lilya**: Ever since I got my papers and an aircraft, I had been looking for a place to fly freely.

**Lilya**: In Zeno, I needed a mission to fly. At the Foundation, my role would've been reduced to a mere instructor. So I looked for a place with endless problems where I could have unlimited fun in the air.

**Lilya**: Can you guess where I ended up?

**Lilya**: Your suitcase.

- **Choice:** I should get going

**Lilya**: Bye. Maybe you can join me for another ride some time.

**Lilya**: Not like the last time when you were sick, but in an actual air fight!

**Lilya**: Zeno? It's a party over there.

**Lilya**: They've been wanting to erase Arcana from existence since 1999.

**Lilya**: And they finally did. The bomb flattened the entire military base, leaving nothing behind. The authorities have officially confirmed her death.

**Lilya**: Now everyone in Zeno is celebrating the victory.

**Lilya**: There was a commendation ceremony. Even the dogs were honored with medals.

**Lilya**: But accolades mean nothing to me, so here I am, talking to you.

- **Choice:** I should get going
