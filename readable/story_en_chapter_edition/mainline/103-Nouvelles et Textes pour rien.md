> **Subtitle:** We smelled food, we heard thunder, we saw the world of freedom. Everything is cheering for you and me.

> **Title Card:** Storm and the New World

**Student B**: Did you hear it? Did you hear it?

**Student B**: It's the rain! It's raining outside!

**Student B**: Climb up the stairs and open the lock ... and then we can get in the watchtower!

**Vertin**: Don't let down your guard. Let me see.

**Vertin**: It's an ordinary iron gate. Through that door frame, everything on the first floor of the northwest watchtower is plainly presented.

**Vertin**: In the oval-shaped room, they see a wall phone, a coat rack, and some randomly hunched batons.

**Vertin**: No signs of people on the first floor. Behind the shutters, lightning flashes and thunder rumbles outside.

**Vertin**: ... That's the gunfire for freedom.

**“The Ring”**: How are the Shamirs with the lock? Oh! Good for them. The door is unlocked ...!

**Isabella**: Shhh ...! Don't get excited now! We haven't gone out yet!

**Isabella**: One after another, keep it quiet ...! Don't touch anything.

The kids walk out of the room one after another.

There's tension in the air.

**“The Ring”**: The guards should be on the second floor. Let's move!

What stands between them and the outside world is a simple wooden door. There's no lock on it. It can be easily opened with a twist at the door knob.

The round keyhole on the door feels warm.

**Vertin**: ...

**“The Ring”**: Relax, Vertin. The best is yet to come!

**“The Ring”**: Wait till we take a breath of the real air ... Till we stuff ourselves with delicious food ... And till we get back to our country ...

**“The Ring”**: There's so much more for us to be shivering at, but in excitement!

**Vertin**: You're right.

**Student B**: Let's open it together.

**Student C**: Three, two, one ...

Their hands feel warm.

**Isabella**: May the freedom be with us!

**Student B**: Let's goooo!

**Isabella**: Vertin ...! Come outside!!

**“The Ring”**: Freedom ...! Long live liberty ...!

They wave at her happily.

There is a gentle push at the back; Vertin finds herself standing outside the door.

**Vertin**: Ha ...

**Vertin**: The first thing that comes to her senses is a moist, complex smell, coming from various kinds of food.

**Vertin**: Then a loud thunder.

**Vertin**: Like a celebratory salute ...

**Vertin**: Meanwhile, people's clothes fall to the ground like confetti.

**Vertin**: At last, a scratchy sound, like something ferrous, falls on the ground.

**Vertin**: ...?

**Vertin**: ...!

**Z**: Vertin.

**Z**: Do you want to be ... the Timekeeper?

**Constantine**: Heh, checkmate.

> **Title Card:** THE END
