## Part 1 · Reckoning Time

> **Subtitle:** Before the dust settles.

> **Title Card:** On Another's Sorrow

> **Title Card:** Reckoning Time

Countdown to the "Storm"

> **Title Card:** Courtroom

**Special Tribunal Officer**: Order, order.

The gavel strikes with perfect momentum, not overly dramatic, but enough to serve as a warning.

**Creius**: ...

**Creius**: The commotion from the jury box yields to a penetrating silence.

**Special Tribunal Officer**: Once again, Mr. Creius, this proceeding is not an "interrogation." But you are obligated to respond to the questions put forth to you by this tribunal.

**Special Tribunal Officer**: Now, please give a full account of your unsanctioned actions proceeding from October 1st, 1920 onward, following the tenth "Storm."

**Special Tribunal Officer**: You are reminded that your testimony must be complete, truthful, and without omission. Our time is limited.

**Creius**: Understood, Your Honor.

Foundation officers and staff watch as he looks down at his prepared statement and begins to read.

**Creius**: On October 1st, 1920, I issued an unsanctioned order to Sergeant Yermolai, a member of my squad, directing him to carry out a mission on the Western Front in Europe.

**Creius**: In addition, during my tenure as head of security in the safe zone, I instituted a "frontline contingency plan" without prior authorization from the Joint Committee.

**Creius**: Following that, I took advantage of the policy approved by the Committee in December—the Resolution on the Expansion of Admitted Persons—and issued admission licenses far exceeding my allowance under the regulations.

The tribunal officer stares right past Creius, looking instead at the representative of the Hawks.

**Irinei**: ...

Irinei maintains a stern expression.

**Special Tribunal Officer**: Then you admit to acting beyond the regulations of this policy in order to distribute these licenses, most of which were issued to military-related individuals. Would you care to explain why?

**Creius**: I believed the Foundation would need them.

**Special Tribunal Officer**: Elucidate, please.

**Creius**: All this time, the Foundation has been sheltering only a small number of core personnel from each era. We've had to rebuild our ranks from the top-down after every "Storm."

**Creius**: I don't doubt we had our reasons. We sheltered only the best of the best. After all, a farmer sets aside their most productive seeds to survive a coming flood.

**Creius**: But we're fighting a war out there. I hardly think there's any need to "elucidate" how this policy has kept us on the back foot. We're scrambling.

**Special Tribunal Officer**: Irrespective of our shortages, you can't possibly claim these people you've taken in are fitting candidates. They aren't qualified specialists; they're soldiers. The only reason they're fighting for us is to protect themselves from Manus Vindictae.

**Special Tribunal Officer**: How do you intend to ensure their continued cooperation after the "Storm" takes us to a different era?

**Creius**: We tell them what they're fighting for.

**Creius**: Every man and woman that fought in those trenches has already come to terms with the idea that they may never see their home again, all for the sake of a senseless war without any hope of an end. What better qualifications could we ask for?

**Special Tribunal Officer**: Would you state that as a matter of fact, or as your opinion?

**Creius**: I speak from my own experience, Your Honor.

**Creius**: Every one of us here has been snatched from our eras. We've all lost homes and loved ones to the "Storm."

**Special Tribunal Officer**: ...

**Creius**: But the Foundation saved us and gave us a reason to fight, not for vengeance but for the future itself.

**Creius**: We are the lucky few who benefited from the Foundation's old policy. I only aim to give that chance to more people. Expanding our numbers will not change our goals. We can save more people, and I believe we must.

**Creius**: Besides that, the truth of the "Storm" will help persuade them—to say nothing of Manus Vindictae. Just think about how many they've already driven to our side.

The tribunal officer organizes her files—an excuse for a brief pause.

**Special Tribunal Officer**: You've made your point. Now let's turn to the matter of your use of tracking serum on a member of your squad.

**Special Tribunal Officer**: First, please provide a factual account of the events.

**Yermolai**: I'll be keeping tabs on her from now on, Marsha. Even got a tracking serum—Creius's order.

**Marsha**: "Tracking serum"?

**Merel**: I-I won't run again. Trust me, I swear!

**???**: Mandrake extract taking effect. Subject calmed.

**???**: Don't sweat it. Her vitals are fine.

**???**: Give us the room, will you? We don't need any help with the injection.

**Merel**: ...!

The elevator's doors open, freeing Merel from its cold, silvery walls.

**Vertin**: Is there something wrong, Merel?

**Merel**: I ... I'm fine. Let's go, Ms. Timekeeper.

**Vertin**: Please, call me Vertin.

**Merel**: Yes, Ms. Vertin.

**Vertin**: Just Vertin is fine.

**Merel**: Oh, er, right. Vertin.

Her voice drifts downward as she stows away her ID card and copy of Visitor Guidance—already on its umpteenth edition. When the elevator comes to a stop, she seems only too eager to hop forward and out.

**Merel**: Ward 1119. Ah, this way.

**Marsha**: Merel? Please, come on in.

**Vertin**: It's good to see you again, Marsha.

**Merel**: Oh, you're looking much better!

The visitors approach their injured comrade.

**Merel**: We brought you some snacks, but security seized them at the door. Apparently, visitors aren't allowed to bring food.

Merel's face turns from downcast to bright and sunny.

**Merel**: But they did let me keep this.

A plump bird appears in Merel's palm.

**Marsha**: A bird? How cute. What species is it? I don't think I've seen such a bird before.

**Merel**: I saw it in The Arcane Creature Keeper's Travelogue. It didn't mention the species, though.

**Merel**: Actually, I think it said that even the author hadn't seen it. The picture was just a rough reconstruction. So I improvised a few bits.

**Marsha**: A reconstruction? Are they extinct?

**Merel**: Apparently. No one's seen one for over 40 years, so it seems like they're gone for good.

**Merel**: *sigh* What a shame! I hope there are some left out there. I bet the real version's even cuter.

**Merel**: I made it from some old bits and bobs I left at headquarters. One of them was a "blueprint" I drew during a maths lesson.

**Merel**: It was supposed to be a flying model powered by arcane skills, but, er, that was a bit beyond my ability at the time.

**Merel**: But, since my arcane skills have improved, I decided to give it another shot. I retooled the design, added a few new ideas ...

**Merel**: And, ta-da! Go on. See what it can do!

**"Bird"**: *chirp*

The handmade bird flaps its little wings and leaps from Merel's hand to the bed.

**Vertin**: It's amazingly lifelike. The arcane skill you cast on it must be sophisticated.

**Marsha**: Thank you, Merel. I'll treasure it as if it were a sacred relic.

She stretches an arm out from the blankets and places the bird beside her pillow.

**Marsha**: Somehow, the way it moves reminds me of you.

**Merel**: I gave it all the healing incantations I could think of. Not sure if any of them actually work, though. Anyway, I thought it might keep you company while we're away.

**Merel**: Vertin and I have been assigned a mission at the SPDM. We'll need to go in a minute.

**Marsha**: Huh? They're sending both Team Timekeeper and the "XII" Squad to the SPDM?

A brief flash of surprise crosses her face. It's hard to imagine someone like Yermolai playing chaperone at the SPDM.

**Merel**: Uh, no, heheh, not the "XII" Squad. I've been seconded to Team Timekeeper for the mission.

**Vertin**: The SPDM has taken in a large number of "Storm" orphans in the last few months, and they're preparing for next term's opening ceremony.

**Vertin**: With our shortages in manpower, they've been recalling any available alumni to assist, including Merel and me.

**Marsha**: I see. I'm sure you'll do well this time.

**Merel**: O-Of course! I'll try my best.

**Marsha**: How is Commander Creius? Paravyan mentioned that the Committee has brought him to court.

**Vertin**: I'm sure it isn't anything too serious. I believe the Committee only wants to collect as much intelligence as possible before the next "Storm."

**Merel**: Yeah. That's why the squad members have all been reassigned.

**Merel**: I asked Paravyan and Yermolai to come visit you with us, but they were tied up. Looks like we'll have to wait till the "Storm" ends before we reunite.

**Marsha**: The "Storm" ... Then the end of this era is coming.

**Vertin**: Yes, but don't worry. The Foundation has already begun recalling its staff, and you'll be safe here.

**Marsha**: The doctor said that as long as I stay in the rehab center, I won't feel a thing when it happens. But it's my first time braving the "Storm."

**Marsha**: Commander Creius walked me through what to expect. He told me I would get "used to it."

**Marsha**: You've each been through it many times now, right? Have you gotten used to it?

**Merel**: ...

Only once she's outside the ward does Merel allow the worry on her face to show.

**Merel**: No one ever really gets used to it, do they, Vertin?

**Vertin**: I still haven't.

**Merel**: *sigh* It's almost time. Shall we set off early?

**Vertin**: Sorry, I can't. I just received a message. There's something I need to handle back at headquarters before I go.

**Merel**: Should I wait for you, then?

**Vertin**: No need. Just go and find Matilda at the ceremony hall. She'll tell you what to do before I arrive.

**Merel**: Matilda? You mean Matilda Bouanich, our old monitor assistant?

**Vertin**: Yes. She's an investigator now—a "star" investigator, she'll insist. She'll also be helping out.

**Merel**: Got it. I hope the school shuttle's still running. I haven't been back since I graduated.

**Vertin**: Take it as a good chance to revisit, then. Sonetto and I will join you soon.
