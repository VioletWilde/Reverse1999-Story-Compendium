## 38510101 · A New Gate Opened

> **Subtitle:** Stay curious and keep rolling. We have arrived in a new world.

A researcher darts through the halls of a silvery-white building, leading his companions to the end of a corridor.

**Mesmer Jr.**: "Stage in the Rain"?

**X**: That's right, a stage that offers an incomparable experience with each observation. How was it last time, Vertin?

**Vertin**: It was unforgettable, being able to travel to an earlier era without ...

**X**: Without the "Storm." Visually, they're quite similar, but the stage's rain is orders of magnitude safer.

**Mesmer Jr.**: I think I'll pass, all the same.

**X**: Don't preclude yourself prior to experiencing it. Who knows? We might just find something to pique your interest.

**X**: We're here.

> **Title Card:** Lorentz Lab

**X**: A whole new world, with an untouched, uninterrupted history.

**X**: I'm looking forward to observing the effects of our interference as they ripple outward into their world.

**Mesmer Jr.**: ...

**Mesmer Jr.**: Her eyes narrow, reading into the young man's visible delight.

The lab is illuminated by a sparse circle of dim lights that surrounds the massive ring-shaped machine.

**Mesmer Jr.**: So, this is the Huygens-Fresnel Interferometer?

**X**: Correct. It allows us to observe an entirely different world using only our minds—the "Stage in the Rain."

Mesmer Jr. steps forward, inspecting the colossal structure up close.

**X**: If you'd like a more thorough introduction to the interferometer, I have an Observation Guide here for you to peruse.

**X**: Although there's no better educator than firsthand experience, it might help to do some supplemental reading before you get started.

**Mesmer Jr.**: I still haven't made up my mind, X. I could refuse, you know?

X smiles. He's heard that one before.

**Mesmer Jr.**: Does this belong to Laplace? This lab, this device ... I've never even heard of this project, let alone used it.

**Mesmer Jr.**: I don't claim to know every piece of equipment in this building, but how was a device of this scale developed without so much as a whisper?

**Mesmer Jr.**: But then no one ever seems to notice when a butterfly flaps its wings.

**X**: ...

**Mesmer Jr.**: Your pretense for inviting me here was to help you monitor how the stage affects brain activity.

**Mesmer Jr.**: That much makes sense to me, given my research on Artificial Somnambulism.

**Mesmer Jr.**: But if I join this project, will it be for YOUR benefit, or ...

**Lucy**: My apologies for arriving behind schedule.

The clang of high-density metal striking the floor echoes throughout the room as the Awakened approaches.

**Mesmer Jr.**: Madam Lucy?!

**X**: Oh, this is an unexpected variable.

The conversation shifts immediately to the arrival of this unexpected guest.

**Vertin**: Forgive me, X. I was the one who invited Madam Lucy. After our last experiment, I shared some of the details with her.

**Lucy**: Using Chaos Particles and the observer's consciousness to simulate a hyper-realistic space ...

**Lucy**: I would be remiss not to participate in such an experiment given its potential to elucidate the inner workings of Awakened consciousness.

**Lucy**: This research presents a wealth of opportunity, not just for a single person or project, but for all of Laplace and researchers everywhere.

Her welded metal face scans over the machine, light pulsing from her optical array—a means of expressing thoughtfulness.

**X**: Superlatively said, Madam Lucy.

**X**: I hope that puts some of your doubts to rest, Mesmer.

He blinks innocently at Mesmer.

**X**: Now, let's move on to the details of our experiment. The interferometer has undergone some improvements based on the data we gathered last time.

**X**: We can utilize Chaos Particle projections to display some probable locations from the environment we'll encounter while immersed in the stage.

X activates the interferometer's console. Soon, locations flash across the screen—a plaza, a laboratory, a bedroom ...

**Mesmer Jr.**: The iconography suggests it's somewhere in the Soviet Union? But the technology seems wildly different from our own timeline.

**X**: It would be more accurate to think of it as a potential version of it.

**X**: This is the "Stage in the Rain" we'll be exploring this time. From the preliminary readings, it appears that it is indeed the Soviet Union, in a timeline that has undergone greatly accelerated advancements in technology.

**X**: A world that champions rationality and order, and one without arcanists. Sounds interesting, don't you think, Mesmer?

**X**: Besides, Vertin and Madam Lucy will be there with you.

**Mesmer Jr.**: ...

**Mesmer Jr.**: I'll admit, I am curious.

**X**: Phew, that's great. I'll do my utmost to orchestrate things from here. While there's undoubtedly a lot to learn about this machine, I believe I should be able to troubleshoot any problems you encounter.

**Lucy**: Researcher X, may I propose that you allow me to operate the machine instead?

**X**: Hmm? But you aren't familiar with its operation, are you?

**Lucy**: I should be able to perform adequately based on your demonstration. Should I encounter any trouble, I trust that as a researcher, you have kept a thorough operation log to consult.

**Lucy**: I will be able to learn much more about the interferometer's functionality and the theory behind it if I am operating it directly. The knowledge I gain from it may prove beneficial to other avenues of research in Laplace.

**Lucy**: I intend to relay any data gathered here to Researcher Adler when I see him next.

**Vertin**: Given Madam Lucy's computing power, I'm certain she can resolve any problems we encounter, and it may give Ms. Mesmer some peace of mind.

**X**: Looks like the decision's been made for me.

X mulls over his words, well aware that if he gives up the controls, it will be nearly impossible to hide Institutum Lorentz's secrets.

But then he finds himself looking toward the interferometer. Despite having worked on it for so long, he hasn't ever experienced it for himself.

**X**: An untouched, uninterrupted history.

Affecting the world around you is the best proof of your existence. He can personally attest to that.

X smiles.

**X**: Alright, I acquiesce to your request, Madam Lucy. Take the reins.

Following the Awakened's command, the drizzle of raindrops falling within the interferometer halts. Droplets are shivering static in midair.

**Lucy**: Interferometer activation complete.

**X**: Looks like you've got a handle on it already, boss. Oh, another thing—

X pulls out a communication device resembling a pager.

**X**: This will allow us to communicate while inside of the "Stage in the Rain." A little improvement based on feedback from last time.

**X**: If we encounter any problems during the experiment, we can use it to contact you.

She takes the communicator from X.

**Lucy**: Very well. Please be prepared. The curtain is about to rise.

**X**: Thank you, Madam Lucy. I'll endeavor to bring you back a souvenir.

**Lucy**: I will look forward to it. Have a safe journey.

**Scientist I**: Each component hard-wired to preserve its collective superstructure ... resistant even to the erosion of time itself.

**Vertin**: Though it looks like the machine decided that you and X already pass for normal here.

**X**: I have to say, it's an unusual circumstance when being dressed as a Laplace researcher renders someone more inconspicuous.

As the last few mountains blur past, the view outside opens up into a wide plain.

**Mesmer Jr.**: Those fields look endless.

**Vertin**: It seems so peaceful.

**Maxim**: That much is clear. Where should I start? Oh, of course, let's start with Kollektiv.

**Maxim**: All the robots you see are controlled by the Kollektiv Neural Network, which ensures their labor is never wasted.

**Maxim**: Who would've thought that robots would become our closest companions? Honest, stable, graceful, calm. They give each and every comrade their undivided attention.

**Vortex**: Attention, passengers. The train is now arriving at its final stop. Please collect your vehicle activation code from Rafik, and a Turbine will transport you directly to the site of the International Polymer Summit.

**Vortex**: Please alight the train in an orderly fashion. We hope to see you again soon aboard the Vortex.

**Mesmer Jr.**: Is that scientist drunk?

**Ethan Brown**: Kids? Why are these kids here? How old are you?

**Ethan Brown**: No one told me we could bring our families to the summit. Or did we get on the wrong train, and now we're headed to a Young Pioneer's camp?

**Alexei**: Comrade, I think these young people are scientists like us.

**Ethan Brown**: Scientists? Them? Apurrhahaha ...

**Mesmer Jr.**: At least we had the good sense not to drink ourselves silly before an important conference.

**Ethan Brown**: Young people these days, always in a rush. You should be at home enjoying the summer, playing on the swings ...

**Alexei**: You're being impolite. Our companions are waiting outside. Come with me!

He cuts off the rest of the man's ramblings and stumbles toward the exit while supporting his drunken friend.

X runs up to them with an apple in his hand.

**X**: Hold on. Why don't you have some fruit? He looks like he could use it.

**Alexei**: Th-Thank you. I'm sorry for his behavior.

The scientist picks up his pace in dragging his colleague away from the embarrassing scene.

**Mesmer Jr.**: Alcohol can have medicinal benefits in proper dosages.

Maxim furrows his brows, visible frustration on his face. He pats X on the shoulder.

**Maxim**: Don't take any of that to heart, friends. Next stop, the exhibition hall, and welcome to Facility 3826!

**Maxim**: This way. That's Rafik, the one in charge of the activation codes.

As Maxim approaches, the robot's optic sensors begin blinking rapidly, mimicking a human's skeptical squint.

**Rafik**: Requisition approved. Here's your vehicle activation code. Have a safe trip.

Maxim leaves them with a wave. Rafik turns to the young visitors and starts scanning them for identification.

**Mesmer Jr.**: Wait, our identities ...

**X**: Nothing to perspire over. We won't need any scanning, Rafik. I've already got a key.

X waves his hand, dismissing the robot's scanning eyes. Then, in a flash, he produces a vehicle activation key from his pocket.

**Mesmer Jr.**: Where did you get that?

**X**: It always pays to be prepared and to keep your eye sharp. This is the International Polymer Summit after all.

**Vertin**: I assume that means you took it from someone else?

**Mesmer Jr.**: Wait, when you gave them that fruit, did you ...

**X**: Shhh. All I did was facilitate a hands-on experience in the importance of politeness.

**Rafik**: Goodbye. Please have a safe trip.

**Turbine**: Please secure your seat belt. We wish you a very pleasant journey!

Six steel cables shoot out from the Bumblebee and attach to the Turbine, lifting the vehicle into the air.

**X**: If I'm not mistaken, our conference venue is on some kind of floating island. Fascinating.

**X**: Come on. Why don't we check out some of the local music!

**Vertin**: This place really is like what the prediction indicated—a world that champions science, rationality, and order. Wouldn't you agree, Mesmer?

**Vertin**: I think you might like it here after all. Think of it as a paid vacation.

**Mesmer Jr.**: I won't deny the appeal.

**Mesmer Jr.**: But since I'm here with you two, I'd call this a business trip at best.

**X**: I propose we look at it as an academic exchange, the kind with complimentary refreshments, featuring the illustrious Dr. Vertin, Dr. Mesmer Jr., and Dr. X.

**Vertin**: I think I'm the only one that feels out of place here, "Doctors."

**X**: Ah, well, here's to a pleasant trip.
