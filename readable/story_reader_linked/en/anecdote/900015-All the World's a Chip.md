Sight and sound blur into a thick, dark chaos. A few stray beams of light manage to penetrate, only to be twisted and scattered by the turbulence of the water.

She needs the pain.

**Centurion:** "Ahh!"

Or rather, it's a pain she has long expected.

She stops resisting. She allows herself to cough, then forces her racing heart to calm, pulling herself back from the edge of a blackout.

She twists her wrists, using the water's weight to work the chains loose.

**Centurion:** \*exhale\*

The more deadly the situation, the calmer she becomes.

After all, this is a rare, truly genuine "performance."

Centurion flexes her wrists, resetting bones that have dislocated. Her fingers explore the iron walls, searching for a weak spot.

**Centurion:** "Not here. No gap wide enough around."

**Centurion:** "Unless I've landed right on the lakebed. Then there'd be a sharp rock nearby, probably at two o'clock from here."

**Centurion:** "Come on, you mangy tin can. These corners are way too thick."

She searches every angle of the box but finds no clear way out.

Her lungs feel like they're on fire, burning up the last vestiges of oxygen in her system.

But it comes with a new exhilaration.

It rushes from her spine to her limbs, forcing her to pause and fully savor the feeling.

In the darkness, she relaxes her body, floating within the water-filled iron box.

***

***

Sound inside the iron box comes through heavy and muffled. A faint light seeps through the seams around the door, refracting and projecting fractured patterns onto the walls.

Her eyes track the light, up, to the side, and down toward the floor.

Wait.

Near the bottom, a sliver of light flickers. It bends where it meets a specific metal plate.

A hidden door! They managed to slip a new trick into her act!

**Centurion:** "Ha! Perfect."

She pushes off the wall, pressing upward against the ceiling.

She twists around in the water and kicks down hard at the metal plate.

With a dull thud, blinding light and water rush toward her.

**Centurion:** "Ah, she said she lived in Westside, didn't she?"
