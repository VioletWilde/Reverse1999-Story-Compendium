## 90001001 · A Newcomer's Guide

<!-- uttu-body:start -->
7:30Clear

Intern Dormitory, Laplace

***

***

Intern Dormitory, Laplace

**Eagle:** "I got a shiny badge from Laplace. They say the ones for official researchers are even prettier."

**Eagle:** "Still, having even this much makes me happy. I wish you could see me wearing it."

**Eagle:** "Mom, I miss you so much."

The girl's hand stops.

And she opens the Resume she's prepared to submit to the Laplace Staff Committee.

Conference Room, Laplace

***

***

An unused conference room at Laplace serves as the venue for the interns' welcome party.

The setup is simple. Someone has put an automatic coffee machine in the corner, offering everyone as much free black coffee as they like.

The interns flock together, chattering about what lies ahead. Eagle, however, keeps to herself in the corner. She's no stranger to Laplace or its researchers.

Like the one standing at the podium right now.

**X:** "Welcome to the Laplace Scientific Computing Center."

**X:** "The Staff Committee has asked me to give a speech of at least 30 minutes to paint you all a beautiful vision of Laplace's future."

**X:** "But I think your handbooks cover that better than I can." He flips through the notice as he speaks.

**X:** "What's next ... Go over the latest Security Guidelines? I wasn't aware we even had those."

**X:** "Sheesh. We'll be here all week if I read all these out." He tosses the guide aside without a second glance.

**X:** "Let's cut straight to the point—introducing you to your future project teams!"

The interns straighten up without thinking, as if sitting a little taller might somehow bring them luck.

As names are called one by one, some interns light up at the mention of high-profile, well-funded projects, while others slink back to their seats with slumped shoulders.

**X:** "Next up ... from Washington, D.C., Eagle."

As Eagle gets to her feet, she catches X winking at her.

**X:** "You'll be stationed at the 'Storm' Observation Center as an assistant to Marguerite, one of our meteorological observers."

**Eagle:** "G-Got it!"

**X:** "Unfortunately, she's busy with Foundation reports right now, so a tour might not be possible."

**X:** "Either way, your first assignment has already arrived. Once the party's over, start packing for fieldwork. No time to waste."

Eagle feels the other interns' eyes on her, colored with a mix of surprise and sympathy. She sighs, then tentatively attempts a wink back at X.

**Eagle:** "Understood. Should I pack something warm?"

**X:** "Best cover all bases." He smiles.

Canteen, Laplace

***

***

As noon descends, the Laplace canteen is packed with interns gathered in clumps, sharing what little information they know.

**Intern I:** "Does anyone know anything about Doctor Olaf's project?"

**Intern II:** "Oh, me! A senior in my lab said that Doctor Olaf's portable rapid-casting research is one of Laplace's top-priority projects."

**Intern II:** "It has its risks, though. Some people got turned into Carbuncles. But it's no big deal. Doctor Olaf's changed them all back within 24 hours."

**Intern III:** "What about Professor Nicholson? Anything from your senior about him? The look on his face when I checked in this morning was ... something."

**Intern II:** "You got assigned to Nicholson? Well, let me put it this way." This intern, who seems to know everything about everyone at Laplace, gives his colleague a sympathetic pat on the shoulder.

**Intern II:** "Enjoy your meal while you can. Pretty soon, all you'll be tasting is Critter saliva."

**Intern II:** "That said, I did hear his Critter biochemical materials project is coming along well. Stressful, sure, but it's a project with real prospects, better than being buried in Foundation paperwork, anyway."

The interns all turn to Eagle, the newest member of the "Storm" Observation Center.

**Intern II:** "If you just want to lay low and avoid the 'Storm,' it's a fine place for that. But I'd like to think we all came to Laplace with some scientific goals in mind."

**Eagle:** "What's the 'Storm' Observation Center like?" She asks quietly.

**Intern II:** "Your tutor's Marguerite, right? I've heard she's lovely. The type who sends New Year's cards to her colleagues. As for everything else, well ..." The intern falls silent.

**Intern II:** "Apparently, the Foundation has majorly stuck their fingers in the 'Storm' Observation Center's pie. More of a documentation center than a lab these days."

**Eagle:** "I heard she's buried in reports right now."

**Intern II:** "Of course she is. Nowhere in Laplace has more Foundation bureaucracy than that place. For the checkered gray-and-white, you're only as valuable as your word count. And they love sending people out on pointless fieldwork. So, where are they shipping you off to?"

**Eagle:** "Manchester. In England."

**Intern II:** "Look, don't tell anyone, but there's a reason the 'Storm' Observation Center has been so on edge. Laplace just got another one of those letters."

**Intern I:** "From who? Foundation higher-ups?"

**Intern II:** "Bigger than that. My senior told me someone called 'Lorentz Butterfly' has been making trouble for Laplace. She's from that secret society—Institutum Lorentz!"

**Intern III:** "I know that name! She wrote Samplings of Global and Regional Chaotic Energy Route Changes. Her ideas on the 'Storm' are entirely different from Laplace's mainstream thinking."

**Intern II:** "More toward arcanum, if we're being honest. In fact, I bet the data in that paper came straight from Laplace's own sampling sites."

**Eagle:** "Laplace's sampling sites? How would she manage that?"

**Intern II:** "Who knows."

**Eagle:** "Judging by the name alone, Ms. Lorentz Butterfly must be quite a mysterious, elegant person."

**Intern II:** "Ha! Elegant? Every paper she sends is littered with cutting remarks."

**Intern II:** "Apparently they're so beautifully brutal that more than one researcher has had to take a trip to the medical office for blood pressure meds after getting one."

**Intern II:** "People have just assumed she's a woman—I guess because of the butterfly connection—but the truth is, no one really knows who or what 'Lorentz Butterfly' actually is. It could be an Awakened antenna for all we know."

**Eagle:** "What I meant was ..."

As Eagle searches for the words, a figure slips into the crowd, doing their best to go unnoticed—a task made impossible by their brightly colored clothing.

**Regulus:** "Hey, we badmouthing the Foundation? 'Cause count me in!"

**Eagle:** "You're Ms. Regulus?"

**Regulus:** "That's me! Oh wait, you're Eagle, right? I've been looking for you for ages! I'm an intern here too. We should look out for each other, don't you think?"

**Intern II:** "I know you. You're that Regulus, the one who's never managed to pass the final assessment."

**Regulus:** "Ahem. That's because I love being an intern!" She waves it off. "But never mind that. Eagle, you're heading to Manchester, right? Well, great news. I'm your partner!"

**Regulus:** "Well, to be crystal clear, I'm here because Ms. Butte—uh, Ms. Marguerite—asked me to assist you. That's about as far as my plans go."

**Eagle:** "It's good to be working with you, Ms. Regulus. But do you really need all those suitcases for such a short field assignment?" She glances at the four luggage bags behind Regulus.

**Regulus:** "Hehe, Manchester is just crawling with legendary bands right now. So this Pirate's gonna be on the hunt for some serious plunder. I mean, picking up a bit of merchandise while we're there."

#### Notes · Resume

Because of her age, Eagle never attended the SPDM, instead moving between various schools across different eras. From the East Coast to the West Coast, then to Detroit and on to Pittsburgh, her classes were far from steady. What she learned was scattered, limited by the knowledge of each time. Still, with help from her teachers and friends, she graduated with top grades. She sees it as proof of her own capability rather than academic success. Her only regret is never making it into the Scouts. This seemed to be her destiny, no matter when or where she lived. Looking at the recent photo on her resume, Eagle notices the childishness in her face seems to have faded somewhat. She wishes quietly that she were just a little taller.

#### Notes · Security Guidelines

Laplace Scientific Computing Center · Intern Security Guidelines (2002, 17th Edition) ... Section 4: Laboratory Rules (698 in total) ... 172. Sharing unapproved arcane candy, drinks, or alcohol with test subjects, Critters, the beagle, or the substitute director is prohibited. 173. Casting any incantations or rituals that may cause personal injury, damage, or destruction during games with colleagues is prohibited. 174. Using unapproved machines, devices, or mediums with unknown or potential effects to heat your lunch is prohibited. 175. Bringing Critters to the sixth floor is prohibited, especially around Mr. Ludwig's office, which is currently under repair due to fire damage. ...

#### Notes · 'Storm' Observation Center

Built after the fourth "Storm," Laplace's "Storm" Observation Center tracks global "Storms," logs data, and pinpoints their critical points. Laplace can only locate the critical point of a "Storm" once the Timekeeper starts the countdown. Thanks to the Foundation's "Storm" Personnel Reform Initiative, the center now reports live to the Foundation. And yes, the paperwork is endless.

## 90001002 · Putting It Into Practice

<!-- uttu-body:start -->
14:00Snow

Street, Manchester

***

***

Street, Manchester

Nineties Manchester is alive with rock music, leather jackets, and aimless youth, none of which concerns the three researchers on the rooftop.

A bitter wind blows the falling snow through the air and onto their chilled faces.

To be precise, onto the faces of two shuddering Laplace interns and one dispirited Foundation employee.

**Regulus:** "This weather is killing me! I should've known better than to expect a meteorologist's sampling sites to be inside!"

**Eagle:** "I have a scarf long enough for two, Ms. Regulus. You can share it with me if you like."

**Regulus:** "Achoo! Cheers. But you know what would really warm us up? Some music!"

**Foundation Staff:** "Not to be rude, Researcher Regulus, but what exactly are you here for?"

**Regulus:** "You're right. One Laplace intern is definitely enough to handle data sampling. But what can I say? Working under Ulrich has turned me into a certified workaholic. I'm just volunteering some extra hours."

**Foundation Staff:** "Was that supposed to be a joke?" The employee, who has no idea who this so-called Ulrich is, turns to the quiet girl on his other side. "You're Eagle, right? First day and they've already sent you out on fieldwork?"

**Eagle:** "Yes. I haven't even had the chance to meet my tutor yet."

Before she can say more, the equipment beside them lets out a ready signal.

**Eagle:** "Equipment checked, no anomalies. According to the records, the last data retrieval was logged by both Laplace and the Foundation."

**Eagle:** "Ready to begin on your word, sir."

**Foundation Staff:** "Confirmed. The sooner we're done with this, the better!"

**Regulus:** "Too right, it's bloody freezing out here! Hey, Joe, there's a stellar chippy nearby. 18 dipping sauces, they say. Want to check it out?"

**Foundation Staff:** "Is it the one just around the corner? I love that place. Their homemade sweet chili sauce is to die for."

**Regulus:** "We're just standing around anyway. We might as well ..."

**Foundation Staff:** "And we'd be out of this wind. Standing out here is no good for anyone."

**Eagle:** "Ahem. We can go after the sampling is done. Regulations require at least two people on site during collection."

**Foundation Staff:** "Yes, you're right. We'd better stick to the rules."

**Regulus:** "Come on, don't be such a goody-goody. We're better workers when we're not freezing, aren't we?"

**Regulus:** "Just imagine it: coming in from the cold and biting into a delicious piece of battered cod—the crisp outside, the soft, flaky center—"

The Foundation employee swallows.

**Foundation Staff:** "How about I run and grab some? Not the same as eating inside, but better than standing here on an empty stomach."

**Regulus:** "No fair! I'm coming with you!"

**Eagle:** "You can't! Regulations say you have to stay—"

Regulus heaves a long sigh and pushes her sunglasses up in defeat.

**Regulus:** "Ugh, fine. I never can stand going on missions with you model students. Joe, my fish and chips are in your hands."

The employee bolts from the rooftop, disappearing from sight as he makes a beeline for the fish and chip shop.

**Eagle:** "He's out of visual range. I've checked the area with my arcane skill. No one's watching us."

**Regulus:** "Tidy! Then let's get to work."

Regulus produces a data box and connects it to the sampling equipment. As the data begins transferring, an image of the Original Butterfly dances across the progress bar.

***

***

**Eagle:** "Ms. Regulus, I didn't expect you to actually be here to work."

**Regulus:** "Of course I am! I wouldn't have lasted in Lorentz just as long as you if I weren't doing my bit! Plus, this happened to be in Manchester, so I thought I'd kill two birds with one stone."

**Regulus:** "Seriously, do you have any idea how lucky you are to be in this city in the 1990s? The music coming out of this place right now is bonkers! Once we're done, I'm spending at least three days just hanging around the music bars."

**Regulus:** "Though, I should probably spare a little time to get something for the others at Lorentz. I bugged Marguerite and X for ages for them to put me on this assignment. A postcard should do the trick."

**Eagle:** "I've been meaning to ask, what is Ms. Lorentz Butterfly actually like?"

**Regulus:** "What? Haven't you been working as her assistant this whole time?"

**Eagle:** "Only remotely, before I joined Laplace. I've never actually met her in person."

**Eagle:** "But she's always been kind and patient with me. She's never once made me feel stupid for asking anything."

**Regulus:** "Never? That just means your questions are odd at most, not stupid."

**Regulus:** "Anyway, I wouldn't worry about it. She's always been pleased with your work."

**Eagle:** "But I never even passed the Scout assessment. If anything, I've been more like a student learning Chaos Theory from her than a proper assistant."

Eagle stops mid-sentence.

**Eagle:** "Wait. That Foundation employee is heading back! But, by my calculations, he shouldn't even be halfway done yet. Did he catch on to something?"

**Regulus:** "There's no way. We were proper careful."

***

***

Both of them look at the butterfly on the progress bar. It's nowhere near the end.

**Eagle:** "Can we pause it?"

**Regulus:** "Not that I know of. And X never told me what happens if I disconnect it early. Worst case, we leave a trace on the Foundation's equipment."

**Eagle:** "Judging by Joe's pace, the route, and the weather, he'll be here in 1 minute and 32 seconds. The data transfer has 2 minutes left."

**Regulus:** "I'll go stall him!"

**Eagle:** "No. The equipment requires two people on-site to keep running."

**Eagle:** "There's only one option." The two exchange a look.

Meanwhile, poor Joe has made it up the stairs with a handful of hamburgers and a belly full of complaints. He pushes open the door to the rooftop.

**Foundation Staff:** "Their fryer broke down, so I had to—"

A snowball hits him square in the face.

**Regulus:** "I've had enough! I didn't join Laplace to freeze to death on a rooftop!"

**Foundation Staff:** "Regulus! I can't see! Did you just throw a snowball at me?"

He barely gets the snow off his face before another one finds him.

**Eagle:** "Researcher Regulus, please, you have to understand! I ... I can't mess up my very first assignment!" Eagle's voice trembles as she does her best to sound fierce.

**Foundation Staff:** "Pah!" He spits out a mouthful of snow. Right on cue, a third snowball arrives.

**Regulus:** "This isn't even a real assignment! It's just an errand."

**Foundation Staff:** "Both of you, knock it off!"

**Regulus:** "Oh, Joe! You're back! We were just arguing."

**Eagle:** "No, we weren't! We were seeing who could hit the door from here." She heaves a quiet sigh of relief.

**Foundation Staff:** "I thought you two were getting along. Anything happen while I was gone?"

**Eagle:** "Nothing. Just someone being lazy."

**Regulus:** "Hey!"

Regulus kicks up a cloud of snow.

The Foundation employee glances over at the sampling site. Nothing out of place, just the scattered evidence of a snowball fight.

**Foundation Staff:** "Would you two stop? Here. No fish and chips, but the burgers aren't bad."

***

***

The moment he turns to the bag, Eagle quietly zips her backpack shut under cover of the flying snow, and the last corner of the data box is hidden.

***

***

Three days later.

**Eagle:** "Somehow, this mission actually took us three days."

**Regulus:** "Well, we did have quite a few sampling sites to visit! I'd say three days is pretty good—gives me just enough time for some shopping once we totally wrap things up!"

**Foundation Staff:** "Here we go again. Alright, we're all clear. Data's been transmitted, and there are no abnormalities." Aside from those two squabbling the entire way, of course.

**Eagle:** "Thank you for your help, sir."

**Foundation Staff:** "That's all for the sampling. I've got reports to write, so I'll leave you to it."

The Foundation employee waves them off and disappears into the snow.

**Regulus:** "Mission accomplished!"

The two share a happy high five.

**Regulus:** "Your acting could use a little work, but you've got real potential as Lorentz Butterfly's assistant. There were even a few times I nearly forgot we weren't really arguing."

**Eagle:** "Sorry, Ms. Regulus, but I'm really terrible at this kind of thing. I hope I get something simpler next time, like tracking bears in the woods."

**Regulus:** "Well, time to head back. Oh, right! This is for you!"

***

***

A guitar pick falls into Eagle's palm, gleaming with a tortoiseshell hue.

**Regulus:** "Don't lose it. That's a limited-edition concert pick from my favorite band! Who knows? You might find a use for it if you ever join a Scout band one day."

**Eagle:** "They don't have guitars in the Scout band. And I'm not even a Scout."

**Regulus:** "Which makes you just the person to introduce them to it, since you're not one of them yet, right?" Regulus winks at her with a sly grin.

**Eagle:** "You're right. Thank you, Ms. Regulus."

## 90001003 · Interpersonal Skills

<!-- uttu-body:start -->
9:00Clear

"Storm" Observation Center, Laplace

***

***

"Storm" Observation Center, Laplace

On the huge screen of the "Storm" Observation Center, various colorful data flicker like starlight in the night sky.

Exhaustion is written on every researcher's face. Yet another day of running on empty.

Heart in her throat, Eagle approaches her new tutor to officially greet her for the first time.

**Eagle:** "Ma'am—"

**Marguerite:** "Ah, you must be Eagle. Pleasure to finally meet you in person. Please, call me Marguerite."

Eagle looks up at the woman. The smile on her face is gentle, almost flawless, and yet somehow it does nothing to ease her nerves.

**Eagle:** "Understood, Ms. Marguerite. Have you had a chance to look over the data I submitted?"

**Marguerite:** "I have. Very meticulous work. Well done, Eagle. Being sent into the field on your very first day must not have been easy."

**Eagle:** "I ... I just did my best. There are probably mistakes in the files! If you find any, I'll fix them right away."

Ms. Marguerite's lips curve slightly.

**Marguerite:** "We'll be much too busy for that soon."

Suddenly, rapid alerts ring out from a researcher's computer.

**Meteorological Observer I:** "John just messaged me. Institutum Lorentz has released their latest prediction on the 'Storm' critical point!" He opens the email eagerly.

**Meteorological Observer II:** "If only their Butterfly would keep the trash talk out of them."

**Meteorological Observer I:** "Ugh! It's hardly fair to gloat about your results when the rest of us are stuck filling in Foundation reports all day!" He tosses the bound documents aside, dejected.

**Eagle:** "Umm ..."

Eagle turns toward her superior. Marguerite's face is tight with concern.

**Marguerite:** "The wording in this email is simply ..."

**Marguerite:** "Take a breath, Finn. What we do here matters."

**Marguerite:** "Every great scientist's research has suffered setbacks. Our efforts won't go to waste. And if all else fails, I always find a cup of hot chocolate soothes my mind."

Her voice is hesitant, yet the sincerity underneath it calms everyone.

**Meteorological Observer I:** "Thank you, Ms. Marguerite. You look after us all more than anyone. If only everyone at Laplace were like you."

**Marguerite:** "No, no. I just hope that one day I can catch up with all of you."

**Marguerite:** "This isn't what things are normally like around here, Eagle, I promise. Don't let that email get to you. You've done quite enough for today. Go get some rest."

**Eagle:** "Thank you, Ms. Marguerite."

**Marguerite:** "Oh, and these are the materials for our upcoming research. Read over them in your time off."

**Eagle:** "Got it."

> **Choice 1：** Check the research materials

Eagle pinches the papers Ms. Marguerite has handed her. A few pages feel thicker than they should.

Had they gotten damp and stuck together? Or ...

Her fingers pause at the edge of one sheet. She feels a familiar pattern.

The symbol of her true organization.

> **Choice 2：** Observe Ms. Marguerite

Ms. Marguerite quickly returns to her usual calm, as though Lorentz Butterfly's provocation has already been set aside.

She turns back to her paperwork, pen scratching steadily against the page.

Sensing Eagle's gaze, she glances back.

**Eagle:** "So, Ms. Marguerite." Eagle mouths the words silently: See you later?

Ms. Marguerite's eyes crinkle with a smile.

## 90001004 · On-Onboarding

<!-- uttu-body:start -->
9:30Clear

Warehouse, Laplace

***

***

Warehouse, Laplace

Following the clues Ms. Marguerite left her, Eagle finds a quiet corner of Laplace. It's a vast room filled with machines of unknown purpose, so seldom visited that mushrooms have taken root in the damp corners.

Eagle leans in to study the machines, which appear randomly placed at first glance, but there is a peculiar energy flowing between them. She reaches out and catches a barely perceptible glow.

She curls her fingers softly, as though plucking an invisible string, and like a veil swept away, the scene transforms in an instant. A line of faded text appears before her.

She steps in—

Institutum Lorentz

***

***

The laboratory is vast and nearly empty. All types of arcane devices are piled in every corner. The few researchers inside are lost in their own work, undisturbed even by the sound of someone entering.

**X:** "Eagle! You're finally here. No need to stress; you'll do just fine."

**Eagle:** "But, Mr. X, I've only learned some basics about Chaos Theory from you and Ms. Lorentz Butterfly. Can I really be considered a member?"

**X:** "By time alone, you're more senior than most of the researchers here. And, believe me, Ms. Lorentz Butterfly is very selective about who she takes on."

**X:** "She'd never admit it out loud, but her criteria for an assistant must consist of at least a few thousand terrifying rules. Honestly, I thought it would take a miracle for anyone to meet all her demands, and yet ... here you are."

**X:** "You've been her assistant for quite some time without a single hiccup. You organize all the messy data she avoids, and you're even managing to study Chaos Theory while getting to grips with a whole new era."

**X:** "I think it's safe to conclude her preference for you is entirely justified. You're a remarkably reliable assistant."

**X:** "Now, are you ready?"

**Eagle:** "Yes."

**X:** "Haha, that's the spirit! And as it happens, your tutor has just returned."

Marguerite strides into the laboratory.

Though Eagle knows that here, her tutor has another name.

**Eagle:** "Ms. Lorentz Butterfly—"

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "There you are, my little assistant. Good."

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "Now. Let's do something that actually matters."

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "Like catching that Original Butterfly fluttering somewhere out there."

On the big screen, the meteorological data Eagle brought back not long ago scrolls line by line.

**Eagle:** "Wait, that reading just now—"

**Eagle:** "There's a significant correlation distribution. I already noted it."

Eagle hands the data to her tutor.

**Eagle:** "Though what exactly the correlation means still needs analysis. I don't have a conclusion yet. I'm sorry, ma'am."

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "No, you've done well." She raises an eyebrow.

**X:** "How does it feel to have an assistant? A real one, I mean, not someone you can only reach via phone call."

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "If all my colleagues at the 'Storm' Observation Center were like her, I wouldn't need to keep writing those taunting letters."

**X:** "Well, she has learned from the best. Though I have to say, writing those letters seems to have become something of a hobby of yours."

**X:** "Tell me, Eagle, don't you find Marguerite's behavior a bit odd? She's the one behind all those provocative letters, yet in front of the very same people, she's all smiles. That goes a little beyond a joke, don't you think?"

**Eagle:** "Well ..."

> **Choice 1：** Knowing how to adapt to one's environment is an essential quality in a researcher.

**Eagle:** "Knowing how to adapt to one's environment is an essential quality in a researcher." Eagle scrambles for an excuse, trying her best to cover for her tutor.

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "Haha—"

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "I don't recall teaching you to flatter me. Though I'll admit, it is fun to watch."

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "I won't deny I have ambition, whatever the field." The words seem to amuse her.

**X:** "And certain questionable tastes."

> **Choice 2：** I wouldn't exactly call it good, either.

**Eagle:** "I wouldn't exactly call it good, either."

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "Straight to the point. You really are my student."

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "But you're also my assistant. So don't worry, we'll get along just fine."

**X:** "I still remember the first time we met. You were on a mission to join the Scouts."

**Eagle:** "It was a search-and-rescue mission. The target was a puppy."

**X:** "And you were the one who found it. Who needs a team of Scouts when you've got a tracking incantation like yours, huh?"

**Eagle:** "But the Scouts are—well, never mind. There's no way I can join now anyway."

Eagle's head droops. Before she realizes it, a pair of hands has quietly straightened her collar.

***

***

When her tutor's hands draw back, Eagle finds a small butterfly badge pinned to her chest.

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "Eagle, this is your reward for successfully completing your first field mission—an Institutum Lorentz badge. The first and only person here to receive one."

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "Those Scout badges are nothing but cheap trinkets. Just enough reward to make children feel accomplished, just enough control to make adults feel powerful."

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "Here at Institutum Lorentz, you will prove yourself in your own way. Become whoever you choose to be."

**Eagle:** "I will, ma'am! I'll treasure it!"

Lorentz Butterfly watches her deeply moved little assistant with a half-smile.

**Lorentz Butterfly:** "Now, back to work. Eagle, could you use your arcane skill to track the movement of the chaos particles in the simulator? Let's go over this data one more time."

**Eagle:** "Of course, ma'am!"

She decides not to ask about the small, dark red stain on her tutor's cuff that she spotted while she was receiving the badge.

Maybe a paper cut, or perhaps just a wine stain.

Eagle turns her attention back to the data.

#### Notes · Original Butterfly

Every arcane phenomenon has a source. Institutum Lorentz believes the era-changing "Storm" was caused by the flapping wings of the Original Butterfly in ancient times, and that finding that primal Chaotic Energy is the key to uncovering the truth behind it. They theorize that years of buildup and change made the energy volatile, which sparked the various strange happenings of the present day.

## 90001005 · Composite Flavor

<!-- uttu-body:start -->
8:00Clear

Intern Dormitory, Laplace

***

***

Intern Dormitory, Laplace

A flurry of message alerts drags Eagle out of sleep on her day off.

**I'm in a bit of trouble. Could you come lend a hand? Coordinates below. —X**

**Note: If possible, bring something useful. Something that can handle a big guy.**

**Eagle:** "What happened, Mr. X? I'm on my way."

Deep in the Forest

***

***

Eagle finds X easily enough in the thick of the forest. But that turns out to be far from the biggest of today's obstacles.

**Eagle:** "Based on your message, I thought you'd stepped on someone's toes." Or that he was dealing with something that walks on two legs at least.

**X:** "Oh, I wouldn't have needed your help for that. So, did you bring anything useful?"

**X:** \*cough-cough\*

The moose crashes blindly through what remains of the already battered machine, then vanishes into the trees.

**Eagle:** "Phew. At least that problem's solved."

X walks over to what is left of his beloved machine. He crouches down beside it, looking rather pained, and picks through the fragments on the ground.

**X:** "Tragic. I never drew up any plans when I built it. And all that smoke ... it's covered us in salt."

**Eagle:** "Mr. X, don't let your guard down yet."

> **Choice 1：** Listen carefully to the surroundings

Eagle listens carefully to the forest around her, the way her father once taught her to.

The moose's heavy hoofbeats are already fading. It sounds thoroughly spooked. But something smaller is still lingering nearby.

No, not lingering. Changing direction—

> **Choice 2：** Carefully observe the surroundings

Eagle scans the area carefully. She knows how to read a forest.

The moose has left a trail of destruction. It's remarkable what a creature that size can do. Not far off, the shrubs sway gently in the wind.

No, not the wind. Something small is pushing through from the other side.

**Eagle:** "Wait! Something's coming." She stops.

**Eagle:** "Actually, never mind, Mr. X. It's nothing." A smile sneaks into her voice as she speaks.

Catching the shift in tone, X feels a sudden unease.

**X:** "Eagle, are you keeping something from me?"

**Eagle:** "It's fine—"

Before she can finish, X feels something wet and rough drag slowly across the top of his head.

He wheels around to find three small moose staring at him with enormous round eyes, happily licking away.

**X:** "Hey!"

**Eagle:** "It's in their nature. They want the salt."

A little further back, the larger moose has returned as well.

**Eagle:** "That one must be their mother. She won't hurt you, as long as you don't threaten her babies."

**X:** "What counts as a threat exactly? Stopping them from licking me? Wait."

The mother moose steps closer, and X finally understands what that look in her eyes meant all along.

She wants the salt too. She extends her tongue—

One hour later.

**Eagle:** "Mr. X, please don't be cross with me."

**X:** "You're going to swear to forget everything that happened today."

X attempts to smooth down his thoroughly licked hair. It does not go well.

**Eagle:** "Alright." Eagle doesn't sound entirely convinced.

**X:** "Still, you came to help. I honestly wasn't expecting you to."

**Eagle:** "Then you really don't trust me."

**X:** "Ha. Whatever you say."

**Eagle:** "Speaking of which, what would've happened if I hadn't shown up?"

**X:** "Neither of us would have liked that outcome. So, thank you."

**X:** "Here. Something for you."

***

***

Eagle takes the small object X has been clutching and examines it curiously.

**Eagle:** "This looks like a piece of copper that fell out of the machine when it exploded."

**X:** "It's not just copper. That is a one-of-a-kind Institutum Lorentz rescue medal."

**Eagle:** "But it really does just look like a copper plate, and it's sticky!"

**X:** "Treasure it, Eagle."

**Eagle:** "W-Wait, Mr. X!"
