## 193301 · Creative Differences

> **Subtitle：** Pending Release

> **Title card：** 10:14

> **Title card：** Office, TV Station

**ONiON**："I'm sorry, Director. I just don't agree with you at all."

**ONiON**："We can't judge what's news by ratings and buzz. It's the media's duty to reveal the truth to the people. They have a right to know what's actually happening in this world!"

**ONiON**："I don't understand why you've refused to approve Headonion as a feature program."

**ONiON**："It's certainly worth more than Hollywood gossip."

**Director**："Worth more?"

The director holds a paper file nearly an inch thick, laying out in excruciating and desperate detail why Headonion deserves a spot as a feature program.

**Director**："Ms. ONiON, are you really that confident that people will care about this kind of thing?"

> **Title card：** Headonion Project Proposal - opened to the page with a photo clipped in

**Director**："For instance, this one, Vanished in the Rain: Farmers on Alert for the Sky Thief."

He holds out a copy of a photo, showing the blurred outline of a saucer-shaped object hovering above ground.

**Director**："Spiral Codes Found in Kansas Farmland: Force of Nature, or Invaders' Plot? ..."

Another photocopy lands in the same spot. Across the boundless farmland, the flattened wheat forms a regular geometric pattern.

**Director**："Soviets Claim Capture of an Anti-Gravity Particle, Washington Caught off Guard: Have They Already Made Contact with an Unknown Civilization? ..."

In the copy, a Soviet man in uniform offers the director a practiced smile, holding up an oddly shaped glass, which, to the naked eye, looks entirely empty.

**Director**："*sigh*"

**Director**："These stories remind me of my grandmother's apple pie, by which I mean it tastes overbaked and so lathered in fat that you'd wonder if there was anything close to a real fruit inside at all."

**ONiON**："Maybe she just needs a better recipe?"

**ONiON**："Most recipes on the market these days leave out the real tricks. All the truly best chefs don't want anyone stealing their real recipes."

**ONiON**："I got that straight from someone I interviewed in the culinary arts. One small change in the ingredients, one shift in the steps, and the whole dish comes out completely different. That's why we need the truth."

**ONiON**："The truth is what Headonion will be built on, sir. It will be a documentary program, dedicated to cutting out the lies and getting down to the naked truth—warts and all!"

**ONiON**："Find the truth, and you save the recipe. Might even work for your grandma's apple pie."

**ONiON**："And when that happens, I can put together a feature just for her and air it on Headonion. A win-win! What do you think?"

The man's patience snaps. He slams the nearly one-inch-thick report down on the desk with a thud.

**Director**："Sometimes I just want to rip that newspaper off your head and check if it's full of water!"

**ONiON**："Of course not, Director. It's common knowledge: a newspaper can't hold water."

**Director**："..."

**ONiON**："If you're looking for something to hold water, there are better options. A glass tank, a ceramic bowl ..."

**ONiON**："Wait ... Director, were you being sarcastic?"

**Director**："Seems your social skills aren't totally hopeless after all."

**Director**："Honestly, I don't see anything that stands out in this pitch. Besides, I've already given the slot to Nancy Hensley's Hollywood Night Life. Take a look at how she runs her show."

**Director**："Clear focus, strong hooks, ratings that stand out in the category, and most importantly, people love it."

**Director**："That's the kind of programming we want."

> **Title card：** 11:47

> **Title card：** Manhattan, New York City

**ONiON**："If all they want is more buzz, I can do that."

**ONiON**："Celebrity scandals, political corruption, shootings ... those are things the people want to see. But are they things I want to see?"

She sits at an outdoor café by the street, her pen swirling in her hand. The project proposal before her is covered in notes as messy and tangled as her thoughts.

**ONiON**："Headonion isn't that kind of show."

Ideal and reality collide into despair. She closes the proposal in her hands and looks out toward the street.

Times Square, New York, one of the busiest corners in the world.

Skyscrapers block out the glaring sunlight in sharp-cut shadows. Crowds of people move like water, pooling at the crosswalk before splitting apart again.

**Host**："Hello, miss. Do you have a minute for an interview?"

She turns to face the questioning voice, bringing all other thoughts in her head to a screeching halt.

A mic, a station badge, an impeccably tailored suit, and a smile that's polite but keeps its distance—the look is immediately familiar.

**ONiON**："What sort of interview are you after?"

**Host**："It's for a new show, Crosswalk Interview. We talk to interesting people on the street about what's going on in the world."

**ONiON**："A brand-new program. Good for you. So, what do you want from me?"

**Host**："No need to be nervous, miss. We're just going to talk. That's all."

**Host**："Like, for instance, we're all dying to know why someone would wrap their head in newspaper."

**ONiON**："I've found that people enjoy the process of uncovering the truth more than the truth itself. So, you have to wrap it up first."

**Host**："Makes sense, I guess. But how do you drink your coffee with that headgear on?"

**ONiON**："The coffee doesn't matter, mister. I'm trying to call attention to the end of truth as we know it."

**ONiON**："Besides, I have a straw."

**Host**："What about your lunch? Can't exactly use a straw for that."

**ONiON**："Is this really what you think people are interested in?"

**ONiON**："You're a journalist. You could ask about anything you want, anything at all! You've got the power to find the truth and expose it for the world to see! But instead, you're here asking me about lunch."

The host sighs.

**Host**："You feel really strongly about that, don't you, miss?"

**Host**："But Crosswalk Interview is an entertainment program. We're here to give people something to enjoy at the end of a hard day. We're not trying to change the world."

**Host**："People don't care if there's some greater truth to unwrap. They just want to know why you're wearing that funny headgear."

## 193302 · The Methane Theory

> **Subtitle：** Pending Release

> **Title card：** 12:09

> **Title card：** Manhattan, New York City

**ONiON**："..."

The words might as well be cold water poured on her head.

**ONiON**："Frankly, sir, your show sounds like a complete waste of time and energy for you and everyone watching it—"

**ONiON**："Which only makes it much more painful that this vapid, mind-numbing programming got the greenlight in the first place. Despite it all, I have to give you some respect for that."

**Host**："All the execs ever care about is eyeballs. They're the only thing that matters. More eyeballs means more reach, more reach means more sponsors, and sponsors mean money."

**Host**："That doesn't mean you can't keep chasing the truth, y'know. You just gotta find a way to grab their attention while you do it."

**ONiON**："What does that even mean? Do I have to film a crop circle being formed in real time? Track down a little green man for an interview?"

**Host**："You're asking the wrong person."

**Host**："But if you were asking me, I'd say you're chasing a fading dream. That kind of sensational, hard-hitting exposé stuff is from another time. Maybe you ought to leave it there."

**Host**："Besides, it's a lot safer to ask Hollywood starlets about the latest fashion trends than it is to hunt down conspiracies and UFOs."

He seems poised to say something more, but they're interrupted by a sudden scream.

**Passerby I**："What is that?"

**Passerby II**："This is it! The end is nigh!"

The two trained reporters both turn instinctively toward the commotion.

They find a wide, flat object floating clear as day above the city, small, but unmistakable.

The object flickers with strange, unearthly light.

**Host**："Jackpot! Get the camera on that thing!"

**ONiON**："The Sky Thief has shown up. I knew I never should have doubted Headonion's angle."

> **Title card：** UFO

**Unidentified Flying Object**：An unidentified flying object appears above Manhattan. The purpose of its visit, whether for body-snatching or Broadway, remains unknown.

**Unidentified Flying Object**：Elusive Sky Thief seems to possess a supernatural means of evading photographic evidence, vanishing before any intrepid reporter might snap its picture.

In the distance, the unidentified object begins to tremble and then shrinks from view. It seems to be trying to get away.

ONiON leaps from her chair. The table shudders, and her empty coffee cup tumbles to the ground.

But there isn't a moment to spare for cleaning up. She's already hailing a cab.

> **Title card：** 16:32

> **Title card：** Finger Lakes Region, New York

Hours pass as she chases the object from the streets of Manhattan and beyond, plying her increasingly frustrated cabbie with more and more bills, until at last it disappears from the horizon, leaving her alone in the boonies of upstate New York, strapped for cash and without any answers.

**ONiON**："Last spotted time: 16:32. Location: ..."

She looks around. Not a single sign in sight telling her where she is.

**ONiON**："Uhhh, Nowheresville, New York."

The wind rustles up some leaves and shakes the tall grass in the fields around her.

Her only viewers are a handful of cows, and even they seem more interested in their cud than the existence of extraterrestrial life.

One, though, breaks from the herd to approach their unusual visitor.

**ONiON**："I'm here on the scene of an absolute revelation, and my only witness is a Holstein cow."

**ONiON**："We still don't know what that flying object was, or where it came from, or where it's going."

**DUU-DUU-BELL-BOO**："DUU-DUU-BELL-BOO."

**Holstein Cow**：A special breed of cow set apart by their peculiar call, "DUU-DUU-BELL-BOO."

**Holstein Cow**：These intriguing creatures are our unsung heroes against the extraterrestrial menace, bravely belching out gas to hide the planet from would-be invaders.

**ONiON**："...?!"

**DUU-DUU-BELL-BOO**："DUU-DUU-BELL-BOO."

**ONiON**："Did you just talk, Ms. Cow? Well, 'talk' in a manner of speaking. That certainly didn't sound like any 'moo' I've ever heard before."

**DUU-DUU-BELL-BOO**："DUU-DUU-BELL-BOO."

**ONiON**："DUU-DUU ..."

The bright-eyed cow lowers its head and takes another bite of grass.

ONiON leans over to get a closer look, only to find herself on the receiving end of an impressively long belch from her interviewee.

**DUU-DUU-BELL-BOO**："DUU-DUU-BELL-BOO."

The thick, humid smell of fermentation spreads through the air. The cow keeps repeating the same flat syllables without change.

**ONiON**："—!"

**ONiON**："This is fantastic! I've figured it out!"

**ONiON**："Methane!"

**ONiON**："Scientifically speaking, methane is a potent greenhouse gas. Over a hundred-year timescale, its global warming potential is something like 28 times that of carbon dioxide."

**ONiON**："And cows naturally produce methane through digestion. Just a fact of nature, right?"

**ONiON**："Wrong!"

She scrambles for her notebook and starts writing out her thoughts in an excited flurry of scratches.

**ONiON**："In fact, their methane production is a part of a brilliant scheme for the defense of the very planet! These belching beef-makers are helping to coat the world in atmosphere-thickening gases to prevent extraterrestrial espionage!"

**ONiON**："And why wouldn't they do their part? After all, cows are one of the primary victims of UFOs!"

**ONiON**："This is it! This will be the thing that finally gets people to pay attention!"

**ONiON**："All I need now is a good headline."

As she deliberates between "HEROIC HOLSTEINS!" and "WAR OF THE BURPS," she barely notices as the wind begins to swirl around her—

Until a fierce gust rips her notebook from her hands and knocks her flat to the ground.

She fights to tilt her head upward and gasps.

A massive UFO hangs above her. Its hull is metallic, with blinding lights flickering along the edges.

Not far off, a farmer shouts something.

**DUU-DUU-BELL-BOO**："DUU-DUU-BELL-BOO."

**DUU-DUU-BELL-BOO**："DUU-DUU-BELL-BOO!"

The unidentified flying object seems to sense the cow's cry. It drifts over toward the cow, and a cone of light descends from the center of the saucer, engulfing it.

ONiON pulls out her camera, attempting to capture this astonishing scene.

**DUU-DUU-BELL-BOO**："DUU-DUU-BELL-BOO. DUU-DUU-BELL-BOO. DUU-DUU-BELL-BOO."

The cow becomes frantic, her cries sounding like a pager stuck on repeat.

Bathed in the beam of light, the cow's hooves lift off the ground, and it wobbles up into the sky, carrying a patch of turf along with it.

Then both they and the UFO vanish into the horizon.

ONiON stares up, utterly stunned, then it hits her—this is a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity, the one thing that can bring Headonion back from the dead.

**ONiON**："I've got enough photos now. They'll have to hear me out."

**ONiON**："Soon, Headonion will be on TV."

Her hands shake as she goes to check the film, only to realize, to her shock and horror, that she hadn't remembered to refill her film cartridge.

She had been too caught up in the excitement of the discovery.

**ONiON**："Nice going, onion-for-brains."

**ONiON**："Wonderful, so ..."

**???**："Excuse me."

ONiON turns and sees a man in a gray-and-white checkered uniform.

She's seen this uniform before, at other news scenes. It seems inevitable that these people show up whenever things go sideways.

**Bob**："I'm with the St. Pavlov Foundation. You can call me Bob."

The new-arrival flashes a badge, and the farmer in turn grips her pitchfork a little tighter.

**Bob**："Please, don't be concerned, ma'am. We're an international organization under the Pax Security Council; we're the good guys. Promise."

His reassurance only unsettles the farmer more.

**Farmer**："I thought you secret government agent types wore black."

**Bob**："I'm no secret agent, ma'am, and we don't work for any government directly. We're a non-political organization."

**Bob**："All this flattened wheat, for example."

The floundering Foundation member composes himself, snaps his fingers, and an arcane glow flickers at his fingertips. In the field nearby, the wheat straightens in response, merging back into an unbroken sea of gold.

**ONiON**：—!

**ONiON**："Argh! You ruined my feature! That was the Spiral Code in the field."

**Bob**："Now, on the matter of your missing cow—"

The staff member ignores ONiON's protests, addressing his promise to the farmer instead.

**Bob**："I'm afraid bringing her back simply isn't in my power. Nonetheless, the Foundation is offering to compensate you for your loss."

The farmer's chilly disposition thaws a little at the offer of compensation.

**Bob**："There's only one thing that we'd ask of you: that you forget all about this so-called flying saucer."

**Bob**："If anyone asks, you never saw it. If someone tries to interview you for some tabloid newspaper about it, tell them it was nothing more than some bad weather. Does that sound fair?"

ONiON bristles at his offer and springs between the two of them.

**ONiON**："What are you trying to pull here? This was real! A real, verifiable alien spaceship was right above this field! And you want her to just 'forget' that?"

**ONiON**："And Headonion is not a tabloid!"

**Bob**："If anyone asks, as far as you know, this lady here just came up with a tall tale, something to try to grab the headlines and make her famous. Nothing more than that."

**Farmer**："..."

**Bob**："You thought, 'Journalists are all the same. They never care about the truth, only what gets people talking.' You glanced up at the sky and found that everything was normal—just a few birds passing by."

**ONiON**："I will not stand here and let you insult my integrity as a reporter."

**Bob**："Nothing more than that. Got it?"

**Farmer**："Nothing more than that ..."

**ONiON**："N-No!"

The farmer murmurs his words back to herself, then closes her eyes. Her face goes calm, almost numb.

**ONiON**："You absolute stooge! You arrogant, pig-headed, fascist thug! You're trying to silence the truth! The people deserve to know what happened here!"

**ONiON**："Do you have any respect for journalism? Don't you know it was people like me that uncovered the horrors going on in those Chicago slaughterhouses?! People like you would have us going back to eating mystery meat sausages!"

**ONiON**："That investigation led directly to the passage of the Pure Food and Drug Act. The reason any one of us can trust the food we eat is because of journalists like me, Mr. ... Mr. Bob!"

**ONiON**："So go ahead! Whatever you want to slander me with, I'll wear that badge with pride! It's 'muckrakers' like me that bring the real truth to light!"

**ONiON**："We're the ones that expose the darkness that the rich and powerful turn a blind eye to."

**ONiON**："Power left unchecked is a beast off its leash. Caging that beast is our job."

**ONiON**："And I am damn good at my job."

She breathes out, realizing at this point just how long she had been ranting.

**Bob**："I understand, miss. If you feel anything in this process has infringed on your rights, you're welcome to file a complaint with the Foundation anytime."

**Bob**："I'm only doing my job. I apologize if I offended you or your noble profession."

## 193305 · Fish and Bait

> **Subtitle：** Pending Release

> **Title card：** 10:48

> **Title card：** Foundation New York Branch

She ducks into a hidden corner. No one is here. Finally, her racing heart can slow down.

**ONiON**：*exhale*

**ONiON**："It seems there are quite a few cracks in the facade here. I should be more careful."

Her newspaper-covered face twists with concern.

**ONiON**："But there still isn't enough here to write home about. I need something more."

*tap-tap-tap*

Too focused on her thoughts, she misses the faint tapping sound as it approaches.

**???**："Who's there?"

**ONiON**：—!

Startled, ONiON tries to make herself as small as possible, hoping to stay hidden in the shrubs.

A soft footfall sounds, and a white-haired child appears before her, standing beside the little pond in the lounge.

**ONiON**："You're ..."

The white-haired child watches her without a word. The mechanical device embedded in his eye twitches in time with the other pale gray one.

The Foundation, the child, the school, the news story—all the threads connect in her mind, forming the outline of the whole picture.

**ONiON**："You ... You're that student from the SPDM?"

ONiON bolts toward him. His clothes are gray-and-white too. She becomes even more certain.

**ONiON**："What have they done to you, kid?"

**???**："...?"

**???**："You think I'm a student?"

**ONiON**："I think that the Foundation is involved in some sort of unprecedented conspiracy, not only to mislead the public, but also to brainwash and control students like you."

**ONiON**："Just one of those things would be evil by itself, but both? Despicable."

**ONiON**："Don't worry, kid. I'll find a way to help you. I'm an investigative journalist. I'm going to blow the lid off this whole thing!"

**"Student"**："..."

A faint smile crawls across the "student's" face.

**"Student"**："Miss, if everyone were as brave as you, this world would be a much better place."

**"Student"**："But I was actually just about to do some fishing. Care to join me?"
