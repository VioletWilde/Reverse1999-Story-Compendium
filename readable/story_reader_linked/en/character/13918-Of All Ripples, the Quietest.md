## 1391801 · A Friend's Departure

**Spring Nymph I**：Doris ... Gentle, wise Doris ...

**Spring Nymph I**：She lived her long life in peace. May flowers and flowing water carry her to that quiet shore.

**Spring Nymph I**：Where spring never ends, the sky stays clear and the breeze drifts free, and the lake lies warm and clear beneath the sun.

> **Title card：** Nymph Habitat, Ephaistonos

**Spring Nymph I**：Doris, she ... *whimper*

The eulogy breaks in a wave of sobs as the Spring Nymph standing by the lake buries her face in her hands.

**Spring Nymph II**：Doris lived a full life. She showed us a clear path forward.

**Spring Nymph II**：To seek peace of mind and live a quiet life—that's how we'll reach the same shore Doris has.

**Spring Nymph II**：We're so fragile, yet our lives are so long. Doris showed us the one true path.

The water ripples softly. Doris drifts on its surface, pale petals gathering around her face.

Their beloved guide has closed her eyes, as though she has slipped into an endless dream.

**Spring Nymph II**：Let us sing for Doris. Let our voices carry her to the land of bliss.

A quiet funeral.

The Nymphs join hands, circling their sleeping leader, and begin to hum a soft hymn.

Its call settles over Doris's body like a veil, though she can no longer answer it.

**???**：*whimper*

**???**：*whimper* ... Doris ...

Only one Nymph's voice fails to blend with the rest. She sobs, nearly breaking into open wailing.

**Narcissus**：I knew this day would come, but ... Doris, just thinking about all the days ahead when I can't talk to you anymore ...

**Narcissus**：I don't know how I'll get through them!

**Spring Nymph I**：We all feel the same way, Narcissus. Let's just try to stay calm.

**Narcissus**：Doris, you ... you haven't even seen me win the Aquatic Games yet!

Startled by her poorly timed words, the Nymphs turn to Narcissus with eyes full of shock, confusion, and a flicker of anger.

**Spring Nymph I**：Narcissus! This isn't the time!

**Spring Nymph I**：Besides, since when have Spring Nymphs had any hope of competing?

**Spring Nymph I**：Doris must have told you that. You won't even listen to her guidance!

**Narcissus**：No, Doris never said that!

**Spring Nymph I**：Narcissus! You ...!

She's so full of rage that she can barely speak.

**Spring Nymph II**：Alright, that's enough.

**Spring Nymph II**：Narcissus, we all know how much you depended on Doris.

**Spring Nymph II**：And it was Doris's devotion to a peaceful life that allowed her to stay with us for so many years.

**Spring Nymph II**：At the very least, let's allow her to be sent off in peace.

With the elder Nymph's quieting of the outburst, the funeral continues.

**Narcissus**：...

Narcissus says nothing more. She only wipes away her tears and steps aside.

Soon, the Nymphs join their webbed hands once more and take up the hymn.

Narcissus bows her head, letting her tears fall with the song of farewell.

Tear after tear falls, sending ripples across the lake.

> **Title card：** Beach, Ephaistonos

**Narcissus**：*panting*

**Themis**：I hereby declare the winner of this competition—

**Themis**：Hedone.

**Narcissus**：*pant* ... I'm ... so far behind ...

She's nearly flat on the ground, covered in sand from head to toe as she struggles to push herself up.

Sweat drips from her temples, disappearing into the churning waves.

**???**：A Spring Nymph ...

**???**：Why are you here?

**Narcissus**：*pant* Hmm?

She looks up. The winner is standing right in front of her.

The red-haired sovereign studies the Nymph at her feet, curious.

**Hedone**：I thought your kind only lived in fresh water. In all this time, you've never once taken part in Ephaistonos's competitions.

**Hedone**：Even if you had, your odds of winning would likely have been next to none.

**Hedone**：So tell me—why are you here?

The sovereign's tone carries no contempt.

As she leans closer, it is only curiosity that colors her face.

**Narcissus**：No reason! Hedone ...

**Narcissus**：Lady Hedone ...

Even in her resentment, the sovereign's status leaves her no choice but to add the title.

**Hedone**：Haha! Not a bad answer.

**Hedone**：I hope you do better next time. At least make the rankings, then I'll know your name.

She turns from this minor curiosity and makes to leave.

**Narcissus**：Narcissus!

**Narcissus**：My name is Narcissus! You ... you don't have to wait until next time to know my name, Lady Hedone!

The little Nymph forces herself up, chin held high in defiance. Thankfully, the sea wind is kind enough to carry her name to her ruler's ears.

**Hedone**：Oh, but that's not enough. You'll have to do something for me to remember it.

And she strides away without a second glance.

**Narcissus**：Ahhhh!

She thumps her tiny fist against the sand.

> **Title card：** Nymph Habitat, Ephaistonos

**Narcissus**：Hedone's nothing like what's in the fresco. She's awful!

**Spring Nymph I**：Shh! Shh! Narcissus!

**Spring Nymph I**：You can't ... you can't just say Lady Hedone's name like that!

**Narcissus**：What's the big deal? It's not like she can hear it anyway!

**Spring Nymph I**：How do you know? Lady Hedone's power has no limits in Ephaistonos. Everybody knows that!

**Spring Nymph I**：It's only because of her protection that even we get to live here safely. You'd do well to show her some respect!

**Narcissus**：So which is it? Are you scared she'll hear us talking, or are you grateful she took us in?

**Narcissus**：If it's the first, then I don't see why you're worried! Hedone's got much more important things to do than listen in on us!

The smallest Nymph in the whole clan sticks her chin up firmly, as if stating some plain truth.

**Spring Nymph I**：Ugh! Narcissus, why do you always have to twist my words like that?!

Her companion is fuming, face red, nearly jumping with rage.

**Spring Nymph II**：Narcissus, Coryphaeus Doris is looking for you.

**Narcissus**：I'm coming!

Summoned by the Coryphaeus, leader of the Spring Nymphs, Narcissus brightens at once and bounces off.

> **Title card：** Lakeside, Ephaistonos

**Narcissus**：Doris, Doris! Listen to this!

**Narcissus**：I talked to Hedone today for the first time!

**Narcissus**：And she was the one who spoke to me first! Sure, I didn't beat her this time, but I bet she sees me as a true rival now!

As always, Narcissus rattles off the day's events, glossing over the less-than-perfect details.

**Narcissus**：Doris, you really should've come to see my first competition! Nobody even wanted to set foot outside the lake to support me.

Though she says this, she's secretly relieved Doris never showed up to watch.

**Narcissus**：You're the only one who gets why I'm doing this! It's our secret, right?

**Doris**：Narcissus, my child.

The beloved elder's voice is tender. Narcissus rests her head on Doris's lap while she strokes her hair.

**Doris**：Of course I wish I could've seen it myself.

**Doris**：I would regret missing any part of your growth, great or small.

**Narcissus**：Mm ...

Narcissus's eyes crinkle with happiness. She nuzzles into Doris's palm, beaming at being valued so greatly.

**Doris**：But ... Narcissus.

Her voice softens.

**Narcissus**：That was the day I first knew ...

**Narcissus**：Doris was nearing the end of her life.

**Narcissus**：However long a life may be, it will always come to an end.

**Narcissus**：Ouch!

**Narcissus**：It wasn't a dream.

**Narcissus**：It wasn't a dream! I can ... I can swim in the sea!

**Narcissus**：We were swimming in the sea together! It was all real! It's real, it's real, right?

**Doris**：Shh.

**Narcissus**：But why?

**Narcissus**：We should tell everyone! Maybe we could all go to the sea together!

**Narcissus**：Then they'll stop-stop calling me the most reckless Nymph in the clan!

**Doris**：Narcissus, not everyone longs for the life you do.

**Doris**：They have the right to remain in their peaceful world to enjoy the calm before the storm.

**Doris**：And right now, I simply hope that you become a seed.

**Narcissus**：A seed? Sure! But what do I have to do?

**Narcissus**：Just tell me, Doris! I feel like I can do anything right now!

**Doris**：More often than not, a bird that carries a seed has no clue what kind of plant it will grow into.

**Doris**：So, Narcissus, all you need to do is be yourself.

**Doris**：The real you.

**Narcissus**：Myself ...

**Games Host**：Our next competitor is Narcissus!

**Games Host**：This one's special. The first Nymph ever to stand on this stage!

**Hermes**：Narcissus, my friend! You can do this! We're all rooting for you!

**Themis**：...

**Apollo**：Grief and despair are enough to cause a Nymph to waste away.

**Apollo**：I had to give Narcissus some hope.

**Apollo**：But believe me, my friends, I would never offer false hope.

**Apollo**：Narcissus will have a happy ending. And not only because the oracle suggested it.

**Apollo**：It was she who brought about this change in Narcissus.
