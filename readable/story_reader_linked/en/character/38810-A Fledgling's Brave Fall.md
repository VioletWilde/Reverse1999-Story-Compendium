## 3881001 · Shade Search

<!-- reader-body:start -->
Noon sunlight spills evenly across the room, the freshly painted gray-and-white checkerboard pattern reflecting its soft glow.

The clerk behind the counter pushes yet another document in front of the architect.

**Cornerstone:** Haha, I hope this is the last thing I need to sign.

**Cornerstone:** I'd bet that if we gathered up every document ever signed in this lobby, we could be able to build an entire new Foundation building out of just paper.

She scans the instructions quickly, then signs her name in the bottom right corner.

The clerk gives the application one last careful check.

**Clerk:** Mm, so you'll be returning to headquarters, where you'll submit an enrollment application for a new investigator.

**Clerk:** And during that time, the new arrival will stay temporarily in a guest room at the Nowa Miedź office.

**Clerk:** Since she has no fixed residence, and she needs to ... "adjust to being around people"?

The clerk's suspicious gaze slides past Cornerstone.

It lands on a blonde girl swaying gently in place, oblivious to their conversation.

**Everecho:** Hmm?

But when she notices the unfamiliar gaze on her, her eyes widen slightly.

Cornerstone clears her throat.

**Cornerstone:** I don't think this application will take very long. I've already reported on this newcomer's situation to the higher-ups.

**Cornerstone:** Half a month at most. Once everything's settled, I'll come straight back here and bring her to headquarters.

The girl suddenly pipes up, as if a signal had finally come through.

**Everecho:** Are we leaving?

**Cornerstone:** Yes, just like we talked about before, if you're willing.

**Cornerstone:** We lost you once. I'm just glad you came back.

A faint, gentle smile crosses her face.

**Cornerstone:** You're the same as before and yet different. Perhaps that's a trait of a certain kind of arcanist.

She stops herself, thinking for a moment.

**Cornerstone:** We'll have to look into this properly at headquarters.

**Everecho:** Headquarters ... You said there are so many voices there and many new friends too.

**Cornerstone:** Yes. Over the past two years, the Foundation has helped relocate a few Shades willing to leave this place. Their impressions of their new homes have generally been good.

**Cornerstone:** Now that it's become an office, this building serves a different purpose. It's not so ideal for wandering anymore.

The clerk puts the papers into a folder and ties it with string.

**Clerk:** There we go. Lucky for us, the office just opened. So, they've still got plenty of empty rooms.

Everecho watches the clerk take a large ring of keys off the wall and walk toward her.

**Clerk:** Come along. This way.

Everecho nods to her friend as they part ways.

**Everecho:** Goodbye, Cornerstone.

**Cornerstone:** Wait!

**Cornerstone:** Everecho, do you remember what happened before?

**Cornerstone:** The "Storm," and Olga, and Jasio, who came to visit you.

**Cornerstone:** And Lucjan, who disappeared.

**Everecho:** I only became like this a month ago.

**Everecho:** Before that, when I was what I was before ... Everything I remember of that time is hazy.

**Everecho:** I don't remember them. I want to, but I don't.

**Everecho:** I've asked the Shades here, and none of them know Lucjan.

Guest Room, New Office

**Clerk:** We've only just finished with the renovations, so there's still a bit of a smell.

**Everecho:** Oh, I hope it won't upset my daisies.

The door closes, the clerk's footsteps fade into the distance, and Everecho lies down on her soft new bed.

The hand puppet, having held his patience long enough, leaps off her hand and chimes up.

**Honza:** Everecho, we can't leave!

**Everecho:** Mm ...

She sinks into the cloud-soft mattress, her thoughts drifting away. But this does nothing to dampen her friend's impassioned protest.

**Honza:** This is our home! Uh, sure, we don't know why we ended up looking like this, and we don't remember what happened before. But this, without a doubt, is our home!

**Honza:** Then out of nowhere, these peepy-people barged in, ham-hammering here, bang-bangering there, and now the whole place is covered in checkers!

**Everecho:** But the building didn't refuse this renovation. The stones and the Shades allowed it.

**Everecho:** And once the renovation was finished, people seemed happy too.

**Honza:** Even so, they still can't just drive us out.

**Everecho:** It's not driving out anyone. This is an invitation to a new place.

Her noisy little friend falls quiet for a moment.

**Honza:** Everecho, do you want to leave?

**Everecho:** I ...

A rush of whispers floods into Everecho's mind, drowning out her train of thought.

Leave this place, leave this place ...

**Shade:** Leave this place, leave this place ...

Don't disturb us.

**Shade:** Don't disturb us.

She straightens up and shakes her head.

**Honza:** Everecho, what's wrong?

**Everecho:** The Shades ... They're uneasy, like there's an intruder.

**Everecho:** It's on the east side. A few of the Shades like to stay there.

Old Restaurant, New Office

It's a corner not yet invaded by scaffolding and paint cans, a place that was once a shelter for drifters.

Now it is silent, with only Shades to wander beneath its skylight.

Now, the Shades have gathered together, surrounding a human in gray-and-white checkered clothing standing in the middle.

**Everecho:** Her clothes look a lot like that woman's—the one with the big ring of keys.

**???:** Oh, come on, you should all calm down. I'm just here looking for someone!

A tiny Shade is gnawing at the trespasser's pant leg.

**???:** Hey!

Leave ... Leave ...

**Shade:** Leave ... Leave ...

**???:** Isn't there a single Shade here who can understand human speech?

**???:** They told me the Shades here could talk and sing.

Everecho drifts closer and hums an unfamiliar little tune, soothing the restless Shades.

**Everecho:** Don't be afraid. She won't hurt us.

**Everecho:** Let me talk to her.

The low, threatening murmurs gradually fade, and the Shades clear a path for her.

The unfamiliar woman in the gray-checkered uniform breathes a sigh of relief and looks her up and down.

**???:** Finally, a ghost I can actually converse with!

**Everecho:** Uhm, I'm not exactly ...

**Honza:** Rude! Everecho is no mere ghostie-spirit!

**???:** Hey, I didn't mean anything by it, kid.

**???:** Everyone around here knows this place is haunted. I think most people could go their whole life without ever seeing a Shade. But in this building you practically trip over them walking out the door!

**???:** Though someone said these Shades aren't really Shades at all, but some kind of echo.

**???:** Anyway, since the Foundation established their office here, it seems like they prefer to spend time in these quiet places.

**Everecho:** That's true. We don't often go into busy places, and most of them can't go outside. You should not come here, either.

**Everecho:** We should all stay on our own "island."

The hand puppet shakes his head back and forth, as if vigorously agreeing with his friend.

**Honza:** Island! Yep-yep, now when strangers come to our island, they should introduce themselves! Get with the naming!

**Honza:** Just who are you, exactly? And what are you snakey-sneaking around here for?!

The woman sighs and sits down on a peculiarly lustrous stone.

**???:** Calm down. I'm not an intruder, and I don't mean you any harm, but I suppose I owe you an explanation.

**Zuzanna:** I'm Zuzanna. I arrived at the branch here just last week.

**Zuzanna:** And I actually came looking for someone like you.

**Zuzanna:** The going theory about these Shades is that they're people who develop an obsession with this building in some way.

**Zuzanna:** When they die here, their spirits are bound to it in some way. Is that true? Does it sound like anyone here?

**Everecho:** It doesn't sound like anyone I know.

As she says it, she recalls Cornerstone's question earlier.

**Everecho:** But then, my memory has been fuzzy lately.

**Zuzanna:** Perhaps you weren't around. It would have been decades ago.

**Honza:** Lazy-bones! Why'd it take you so long to look-see?

**Zuzanna:** Oh, I have ... It's very personal.

**Zuzanna:** But this Shade I wanted to find was my cousin.

**Zuzanna:** Her name was Lucjan.

**Everecho:** ...!

Zuzanna said "her," then she can't mean the sickly boy Cornerstone described.

**Everecho:** I've heard the name before, but ...

**Zuzanna:** It's usually a boy's name, I know. Kind of strange to give it to a girl, but my uncle always enjoyed being different.

**Zuzanna:** In her photos, her hair was a lot like yours, one long braid, wearing a dress. I guess that's pretty common here.

**Zuzanna:** Though it was black, and she had blue eyes, not like yours.

The other Shades float around them in the distance, and a faint, hard-to-place unease settles over Everecho.

Like an ink blot spreading across paper.

**Everecho:** I can speak with the other Shades here. We can even sense each other's thoughts in a way.

**Everecho:** I actually asked them about a Shade named Lucjan before, but at that time I was looking for a boy.

**Everecho:** If you really want to find this Lucjan, I could ask everyone again and take a closer look at their faces.

**Honza:** Hold it, holdy-hold it! Why are we getting all busy-buzzied in her business?

**Everecho:** It's just ... Well, Ms. Cornerstone said that we should try "adjusting to being around people."

A flash of realization lights up in Zuzanna's eyes, and she smacks her thigh with satisfied realization.

**Zuzanna:** Oh! I've heard of you. You're the Shade Master the Foundation invited on board!

**Zuzanna:** So, you're going to be staying here for a while then?

**Everecho:** Shade Master? I'm not ...

The now excited woman springs up and steps forward, taking hold of Everecho's translucent hand.

**Zuzanna:** Please! I absolutely must find her!

**Honza:** Hey!

**Everecho:** ...

Everecho closes her eyes, letting the other Shades' thoughts flit in and out of her mind like passing clouds.

They were all born of the echoes in this building, and, unlike her, they've retained their names, their pasts, and their memories.

She meets the gaze of every Shade in turn.

In an instant, names and stories return to her: Oleg, Aima, Anna, Lena ... But no Lucjan.

She opens her eyes.

**Everecho:** No, there's no one here by that name.

**Everecho:** No girl with a black braid, nor even a girl who fell to her death in this building. Nothing matches up.

**Zuzanna:** Are you sure?

**Honza:** The Shade Master would not lie to you! Honza swears it!

Zuzanna lets go of Everecho's hand, and the excitement falls from her face.

After a long pause, she manages to speak.

**Zuzanna:** I suppose maybe it was just wishful thinking.

**Zuzanna:** Thinking it over, I realized there was never any real evidence she became a Shade at all. I just wanted to try.

**Zuzanna:** I wanted to hope, but I always knew it might not work out.

Her words become more like an incoherent string of muttering.

**Zuzanna:** Thank you, "Shade Master." My shift's about to start. I should go.

She waves once and turns to leave, her heart clearly troubled.

Not until her footsteps fade at the end of the hallway does Honza remember something monumental.

**Honza:** Honza forgot to tell her our names!!!

Old Kitchen, New Office

**Everecho:** We will meet again eventually.

A familiar figure stands by the wall, chipping at a gray-and-white stone block with a hammer. Zuzanna doesn't notice the faint sound behind her.

**Everecho:** I'm Everecho. I'm sure all friends will meet again someday!

**Honza:** She's not a friend!

Zuzanna jolts up at first, only to slowly turn around, an awkward smile plastered on her face.

**Zuzanna:** Oh! It's been a few days! Master!

**Honza:** You little thief!

Honza rams headlong into her forehead at the speed of a charging bull. Zuzanna clutches her head and crouches down.

**Zuzanna:** I can explain!!

**Zuzanna:** You'll never believe it. There's a special kind of stone here that can record sounds from inside the building.

**Zuzanna:** The details of the project are classified, but the Foundation actually used this kind of stone to rebuild the dome.

**Everecho:** I know. They're Echo Stones.

Zuzanna looks at her in surprise.

**Zuzanna:** You knew? I guess that makes sense. Ah, never mind. I never asked you anyway.

**Zuzanna:** Anyway, I was hoping that the Echo Stones might have recorded something about my cousin.

**Zuzanna:** They used to have picnics here often, and, well, her fatal fall caused quite a stir at the time.

**Everecho:** But most of the Echo Stones went into building the dome.

**Zuzanna:** Hey! I'm not crazy enough to go smashing up the dome. I just heard there are still some leftover pieces here and there.

She points at the broken chunks of concrete behind her.

**Zuzanna:** They might not be fit for building, but maybe they still hold echoes.

**Zuzanna:** I really think I heard something a second ago! I just want to give it a try! I'll put it right back once I'm done checking, I promise!

Everecho drifts over to the Echo Stones and rests her hand against their pale blue surfaces.

A wave of familiarity washes over her, as if she's done this countless times before.

Whispers begin creeping into her mind.

**Everecho:** ...

**Everecho:** I can hear the voices left behind in these stones, all of them.

**Everecho:** No need to chip any stone loose and take it away. I'll help you.

**Honza:** Everecho!

Honza mutters out an incoherent refusal, but it does nothing to temper Zuzanna's delight.

**Zuzanna:** Oh, this is—

**Everecho:** But there's something I need to ask you.

**Everecho:** The others want to live here peacefully, and your intrusion has made them feel uneasy.

**Everecho:** I'd like you to leave them alone and not come around here from now on.

**Zuzanna:** Sorry.

**Zuzanna:** No trouble at all. After this is done, I won't set foot in your little "island" anymore.

Everecho nods as the Echo Stone trembles in her hand.

**Puppeteer:** The way I pictured her—she'd have yellow braids and a rainbow spacesuit, but, well, this'll do for now!

**Everecho:** Me? You want me to be in a play?

**Everecho:** Was that a dream? Or a memory? It felt so strange. I don't think I was ever a puppet, was I?

Honza droops, unmoving. The overload of memories seems to have taken a toll on him too.

Everecho sits up and looks around.

There's an old man in a wheelchair by the table.

**Everecho:** Good morning.

**Old Man:** ...

**Everecho:** Hello there!

**Everecho:** That's the Foundation's morning bell.

**Everecho:** What happened to me yesterday?

The old man abruptly straightens his neck, as if pulled up by unseen strings.

He wheels himself around to the door and stops just before entering the hallway.

Atrium, New Office

Everecho climbs out of bed and drifts over to meet him at the doorway.

She sees the old man gazing up at the dome above the courtyard, where the same gentle sunlight from her memory is pouring down.

## 3881004 · The Idiot

**Zuzanna:** Morning, Everecho. You're up earlier than I thought.

Zuzanna catches sight of her from the end of the hallway. Everecho's eyes dart between her and the old man with confusion.

**Zuzanna:** Oh, him? He's fine. Follow me. Let's talk.

Zuzanna sighs, wrapping an arm around Everecho as she leads her back into the room.

**Zuzanna:** I found you passed out in front of those stones. I had to carry you back to my room. Lucky you're so light. I suppose that's what being an actual Shade gets you.

Zuzanna walks over to the table and motions to the row of puppets.

**Zuzanna:** This is just a bit of a hobby of mine.

**Zuzanna:** My son loved watching these shows when he was little.

She scratches her head awkwardly, then tries to compose herself by pouring Everecho a glass of water.

**Zuzanna & Everecho:** You—

**Zuzanna:** You first.

**Everecho:** Who was that old man?

**Zuzanna:** That's my uncle. I mentioned him before, didn't I?

A tangle of loose threads combs itself into a thin ray of light.

**Everecho:** Is he looking for your cousin too?

**Zuzanna:** It's hard to say. He's very old, and he suffers from dementia.

**Zuzanna:** He forgets things and doesn't recognize people.

**Everecho:** I don't sense any emotion from him.

**Zuzanna:** Things have been getting worse over the last few months. He barely speaks anymore.

**Zuzanna:** The only words he'll repeat are Nowa Miedź, the building, and my cousin's name.

**Everecho:** Lucjan ...

**Zuzanna:** Everyone just assumed he was talking nonsense. But I thought that if I brought him back to Nowa Miedź maybe it would help him. We used to live here—our whole family, but my parents moved away before I was born.

**Zuzanna:** And thanks to the Foundation office hiring short-term clerks, I managed to get him into this building.

**Zuzanna:** But nothing's changed so far. All he does each day is—

**Everecho:** Go out to sit in the sun in the morning?

She sees her own arm, glowing with light, then a long golden braid, a white headscarf ...

She takes a step forward, both feet touching the ground.

**Everecho:** So, this is where our names came from, Everecho and Honza.

Sound remembers. That's why you never change shape.

**Everecho:** Sound remembers. That's why you never change shape.

Having listened to countless echoes of memory, she now knows where she came from.

She becomes, once more, everything within the sea of light.

She sees Everecho and Stasia's first meeting and remembers the first handshake. She sees the soldiers with their rifles and remembers the workers laboring on the scaffolding.

**Lucjan:** Everecho, you've come back.

She sees a family out on a picnic, the wind lifting the corner of their picnic blanket.

A thin, frail boy takes a bite of bread and breaks into a smile.

**Everecho:** Lucjan.

**Lucjan:** Go.

**"Everecho":** You have to help them. You have to guard their memories.

**Everecho:** Me?

**"Everecho":** You are yesterday's blessing. You are tomorrow's wish. You have already walked through your own memories.

**You are Everecho, and you are everything.**

**Everecho:** **You are Everecho, and you are everything.**

The wind sweeps across the glass of the dome, scraping out a harsh, grating sound.

Everecho steps toward the edge of the triangular panel, and as she does, she spots Zuzanna and her uncle below.

**Zuzanna:** Come on, Uncle, it's time to go.

Spots of light rise and fall in the air, and Everecho opens her mouth.

**Everecho:** ♫ Yes, I am the cosmonaut. I'll carry all your memories with me when I go. ♫

The sound resonates through the entire hall, carried by the Echo Stone, and the other clerks lift their heads.

Everecho draws a deep breath and leaps off the edge.

She falls, like a fledgling bird spreading its wings for the very first time.

**Old Man:** ...!

**Zuzanna:** Everecho!!

Zuzanna sprints in a mad panic toward the base of the dome, but she has no chance of reaching her.

Yet, despite her worries, Everecho drifts down as gently as a feather.

**Zuzanna:** Everecho?

But the girl does not answer the question. She walks past Zuzanna toward her uncle.

She recognizes this face, a face resembling the Lucjan of her memory, a face far more aged than the man beside the picnic blanket.

**Everecho:** Maybe I really did know you.

**Those memories, glowing like light—I miss them dearly,

but they are gone now and will never come again.**

**Everecho:** **Those memories, glowing like light—I miss them dearly,

but they are gone now and will never come again.**

**Will you forget those voices too?

Will you find your happiness once more?**

**Everecho:** **Will you forget those voices too?

Will you find your happiness once more?**

She stands in front of the old man.

**Everecho:** Would you like one more memory?

**Old Man:** ...

**Everecho:** I'm home.

The long-ailing old man's lips tremble.

**Old Man:** Lucjan?

**Old Man:** You're here?

**Everecho:** Yes.

Her hand comes to rest over the old man's eyes.

**Everecho:** Yes, this is a sweet memory.

**Everecho:** It's a sweet dream.

**Everecho:** Sleep now. You'll live on in the past. You'll find your happiness.

## 3881008 · The Cosmonaut

Street, Nowa Miedź

**Cornerstone:** Whew, I think that's everything moved.

Cornerstone shuts the trunk of the car, meeting the eyes of Grundriss as she walks up beside her.

**Grundriss:** I'm not sure it makes much sense to me. She helped the old man break out of his obsession by reenacting a scene from some puppet show?

**Cornerstone:** I have a theory about it, not that I'm any kind of expert in human psychology.

**Grundriss:** Let's hope it's not as far-fetched as some of your other theories.

**Cornerstone:** The old man, who so closely resembled Lucjan's father, happened to also be a father who'd lost a child.

**Cornerstone:** That child happened to closely resemble the puppet version of Everecho, and the cause of death was also similar to the puppet show's ending.

**Cornerstone:** In the process of recovering her own memories, Everecho also managed to untangle the old man's inner troubles.

**Cornerstone:** Was this the guidance of the "Sanctuary of Memories" at work, or just an unintentional coincidence brought about by the "Storm"?

**Grundriss:** The "Storm" doesn't work like that.

**Cornerstone:** Ha, that's exactly why I said that it's only a theory.

**Cornerstone:** Has our friend finished saying her goodbyes yet?

**Honza:** Honza does not want to! But if Everecho wants to go out and take a look around, I'll go with her!

**Everecho:** I ... I'm still not sure if I want to leave.

Olga pats her on the shoulder.

**Olga:** Child, follow your heart.

**Olga:** Nowa Miedź has gotten better, but there are plenty of wonderful things out there in the wider world waiting for you.

**Olga:** You've already done so much for us. We want you to go out and meet others too.

**Everecho:** But there's still so much I don't remember: the dome collapsing, the sacrifice you all talked about ...

**Olga:** It will come back to you in time.

**Olga:** And you are always welcome to come back. We'll be waiting here for you.

**Olga:** Your room will still be here, the Shades will still be here, and we can sit and talk about the old days together.

**Jasio:** It may take you some time, but you've always had plenty of that. The world out there has many surprises waiting for you.

He hands her a stack of loose papers. As she takes them, the unmistakable scent of cigarettes washes over her. He never could kick that habit.

**Jasio:** Spread the word about my poems out there, and come back and tell us what's new.

**Everecho:** Why wouldn't you go out and see it yourself?

**Jasio:** Oh, I think I'll stay put. I've got myself a stable job here.

**Jasio:** And besides, this place is my home now—an eternal wellspring of inspiration for a poet.

Everecho turns around and sees Zuzanna walking toward her from a distance.

Zuzanna had taken a leave of absence after the events with her uncle. The two haven't seen each other before now.

**Zuzanna:** Hey, Everecho.

**Everecho:** Hey, Zuzanna ...

**Honza:** Waity-wait-wait! Did you put the stone back?

**Zuzanna:** Yes, as promised, Honza. Besides, the Foundation has taken over the area to protect the stones and the Shades going forward.

**Zuzanna:** My uncle passed away last night.

**Everecho:** Oh ...

**Zuzanna:** It's all right. I actually came to thank you.

**Zuzanna:** We still don't know what really happened to my cousin back then. But what you did has helped him. I think he died at peace with himself.

**Everecho:** Was he happy?

Zuzanna smiles bitterly.

**Zuzanna:** I want to believe he was. He was smiling right up to the end.

**Zuzanna:** And this is strange to say, but seeing that stunt you pulled gave me a kind of comfort too.

She turns around to look out over the gray city behind them.

**Zuzanna:** It feels like a weight that I've been carrying for so long has finally been taken off of me, but there's still something that draws me here.

**Zuzanna:** Why does Nowa Miedź keep calling out to me in my dreams?

**Everecho:** What will you do now?

**Zuzanna:** Oh, I think I'll stick around for a few more months and take a proper look at Nowa Miedź in spring.

**Zuzanna:** It's changed so much.

**Zuzanna:** But I don't think I'll stay, not forever.

**Everecho:** I'll remember you.

Zuzanna smiles and waves her hand.

**Zuzanna:** You're talking like I'm about to drop dead! We'll have plenty of time together.

**Everecho:** I'm leaving too.

**Zuzanna:** Is that so? Well, until we meet again.

**Cornerstone:** Let's go!

The architect calls out to the girl, holding the car door open for her.

**Everecho:** Goodbye.

**Until we meet again.**

An old memory surfaces from the sea of light.

**Everecho:** You ...

Everecho sticks her head out the car window, looking back at the people waving goodbye.

**Everecho:** You're—!

A name, a name connected to you, a name you once told me, the hand puppet's name, the name of the puppet show's lead role ...

**Everecho:** You're Honza!

**Honza:** Whatity-what?! Everecho, I'm right here!

The figures of the people begin to disappear in the haze.

**Everecho:** I understand now why you're all here. Maybe all of you carry some regret in your hearts.

**Grundriss:** Get back in here! That's dangerous!

**Grundriss:** Whew, I can never figure out how to handle you.

**Grundriss:** You'll end up losing your head that way! Don't you ever do that again!

**Cornerstone:** Hah, our little friend still has a lot left to learn.
