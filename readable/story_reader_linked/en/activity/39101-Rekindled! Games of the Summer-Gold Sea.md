## 3910101 · A Holiday Incident

> **Subtitle：** The distinction between work and rest is beginning to blur.

### Segment 1 · `script 203901`

A classic mix of '90s alt-rock drifts along the sea breeze.

> **Title card：** Aboard the APPLe

Static breaks up the song mid-guitar solo, and a hand shoots for the knob, desperate to tune the channel back in.

**Regulus**：No, no. Don't go. Oh, come on! That was the best part!

**Regulus**：Mr. APPLe! Something's gone wonky with the radio. We haven't drifted into any restricted waters, have we? Should've stuffed Ms. Radio in my bag when I had the chance.

**APPLe**：We've not made any deviations from our course, Captain. We're sailing firmly within international waters.

**Vertin**：Is the navigation system functioning properly?

**APPLe**：Yes, as are all other systems. This APPLe went to great lengths to ensure the ship was in optimal condition before our departure.

**Vertin**：Strange. We should've seen the island by now.

**Regulus**：Far as I can tell, there's nothing but sea from here to the horizon.

The APPLe sails on with only the bright blue sky for company.

**Sonetto**：Our coordinates show we should be arriving. The wind seems to be easing up too.

Regulus shuts off the dead channel, and the noise stops. The faint sound of waves rolls back in, slowly building into a strange vibration.

**Vertin**：Do you hear that?

**Regulus**：Yeah. Sounds a bit like a tom drum on loop with a dotted 8th delay. Hah, pretty nice.

**APPLe**：Were we on land, this APPLe might suggest it were a possible precursor to an earthquake. But I'm not sure what to make of it at sea.

**Sonetto**：Hmm. I think it sounds like a heartbeat.

**Regulus**：That can't be good, can it? Something with a heart that big and pumping that fast doesn't seem like fun to bump into.

A shriek tears through the air, and as if in reply, the APPLe begins to rock against a suddenly restless sea.

**Vertin**：Watch your footing! Hold fast to the railings!

**APPLe**：Captain, the radar indicates unknown objects approaching at 14 m/s—large numbers of them!

**Regulus**：Ourgh, what kind of starting vocal is that?! If this is their attempt at a heavy metal scream, then they're butchering it!

**???**：*shriek*

**Sonetto**：Timekeeper!

The sea monsters swarm across the water in a frenzy, their heads and legs entangled, their numbers alone enough to send a chill down the spine.

A massive shadow surges up from beneath, lifting the APPLe skyward. The ship lists over at an impossible angle.

**APPLe**：Captain, the ship is under severe stress from the tilt!

**Regulus**：Cor! A whale? Please tell me we're not about to be swallowed whole.

**Regulus**：Hold on, Mr. APPLe! I'll take the helm!

Regulus wrestles the spinning wheel under control. Sonetto splays out for something to hold as the ship tilts sharply.

**Sonetto**：Timekeeper, grab my hand!

**???**：*roar*

A thunderous roar tears the boundary between sea and sky apart.

**Vertin**：They're coming aboard. Get ready for a fight!

### Segment 2 · `script 203902`

The Scyllas retreat into the sea and make themselves scarce.

**Regulus**：*panting* Well done, crew. My dear "APPLe" breezed through in style as always!

**APPLe**：We've righted the ship. No signs of flooding in the cabins. The only thing that might be in need of some care is this APPLe.

**Sonetto**：Are you alright, Timekeeper?

**Vertin**：Fine as I can be. Did you notice how those Scyllas all fled in the same direction?

Where the beasts had once emerged, there is now nothing but churning waves.

**Vertin**：I think they were running away from something when they bumped into us.

**Vertin**：Could it have been whatever made that roar?

**Vertin**：Sonetto, how far does it say we should be from the island?

Sonetto wipes away the water from the tracker's screen. The two dots, one for the APPLe, one for their destination, are now nearly on top of each other.

**Sonetto**：It shows that we've arrived. We should be looking at it right now.

Their eyes drift back to the sea, calm again now.

Nothing's there.

One day earlier ...

**Sonetto**：Holiday ... Days off ... Sabbatical ...

> **Title card：** Island Beach

Sonetto shifts her feet over the white sand, bending down to smooth out a meticulously laid tarp as she sorts through the supplies the Foundation is shipping to the island.

She murmurs to herself, weighing the subtle differences between the words.

Beside her, Regulus cradles an armful of cans, eyes fixed on the Abraxas circling overhead.

**Regulus**：None of the above, more's the pity!

**Sonetto**：...

**Regulus**：Don't give me those puppy-dog eyes! I'd have thought a bookworm like you would know the definition of a proper holiday.

**Sonetto**：I understand the definition perfectly well, Regulus. Holiday: A period of time with no tasks to deliver or performance metrics to meet.

**Regulus**：Free time alone does not a holiday make. A beach party would be the least we could do, given where we are. But there isn't so much as a sun lounger in sight.

**Sonetto**：A sun lounger? Ah, Timekeeper!

Noticing her superior back on the beach, she drops the question.

An envelope is held against the sea breeze.

**Sonetto**：Is that a letter in your hand?

**Vertin**：Yes. It's from Ulu.

Inside the envelope is a thick sheet of parchment, its edges lined with familiar scorch marks.

All three pairs of eyes rush to read it.

**Vertin**：Many times have we sought to restore contact with the once hosts of the Aquatic Games, yet we have heard nothing from them.

**Vertin**：Its people came from a Greek city-state long ago. You would have my deepest gratitude should you take this token of their leader and journey to their island home—

**Vertin**：Ephaistonos, as it is now called, and learn how they fare.

**Regulus**：Aquatic Games? That's not quite what I had in mind.

**Regulus**：But just about anything would make for an improvement over this doldrum.

She glances around the white, empty beach and offers a bored shrug.

**Sonetto**：Ephaistonos ... But Ms. Ulu didn't provide us its exact coordinates.

They look over a simple map sketched in charcoal with a dotted line giving them a rough heading.

**Regulus**：Well, that won't do. I may be the greatest captain with the finest ship and the very best first mate, but even I need a heading.

**Vertin**：From Apeiron to the island ...

**Six**：If you departed immediately, you would reach their shores by midnight.

A voice drifts in, calm and unhurried. It's the leader of the School of Apeiron.

**Regulus**：6? What're you ...?

**Regulus**：Have you been there before? Wait, don't tell me: you're a secret sports star, right?

**Regulus**：Never took you for the athletic sort. Hmm, no offense.

**Six**：I haven't set foot on their island nor partaken in their Games.

**Six**：But our ancestors were once one people. Our respective colonists left at roughly the same time.

**Six**：Though the long ages that separate us have molded our islands into very different places.

**Vertin**：Still, you seem to know the way there.

**Six**：Through distant memories of the past—a time long before the "Emanation."

**Vertin**：Then it's possible their island may have been affected by the "Storm."

**Sonetto**：Perhaps that's why there hasn't been any reply.

6 doesn't answer right away. He turns toward the sea beyond the island, the wind catching at the hem of his robe.

**Six**：Much has changed and much endures.

**Six**：Their leader may have found a means to protect their island, as ours did.

**Sonetto**：Timekeeper, look.

Sonetto points to a wax seal on the back of the map.

The seal has an unusual shape and texture, with the outline of rolling waves and an erupting volcano pressed into it.

**Vertin**：Is that the token of their leader?

The moment the fingers brush it, a fierce, scorching wave surges into the mind.

**Sonetto**：Timekeeper!

The flames burn out, and the blazing vision disappears at once.

**Vertin**：—!

Consciousness snaps back. Sonetto pulls the token away with concern.

**Vertin**：There's an archer, drawing a bow and releasing a flaming arrow.

**Vertin**：Wherever it goes, fiery destruction follows.

The blazing heat continues to surge in the mind.

**Regulus**：Maybe the letter was a scam, and we're just the mugs who fell for it?

She leans over the ship's rail, evidently regretting her earlier adventurous enthusiasm, as she scans the distance through her telescope.

**Vertin**：But the letter was certainly from Ulu. And I see no reason why she would lie to us or why 6 would corroborate.

**Sonetto**：Could it be that the island is here, but for some reason, we can't see it?

Sonetto double-checks the coordinates, her brow furrowing as she works through another possible solution.

**Sonetto**：If there's some kind of arcane illusion, then all we need to do is find the right skill to reveal it.

**Sonetto**：But it could be something much more complicated, like a naturally occurring phenomenon or perhaps a powerful ritual.

**Regulus**：Are we even sure the bloody island's still there? Who knows? Maybe it just up and sailed off somewhere.

Regulus lets out a long sigh into the wind, letting her mind wander wherever it wants.

The wind carries her disappointment away and brings back a faint, hopeful voice.

**???**：Are you ...

**???**：... looking for the island of Ephaistonos?

**Sonetto**：Regulus, please. We still have a meeting to get through.

**Regulus**：Right, right. But need I remind you the whole point of this voyage?

**Regulus**：Mystery island or not, this is still a holiday.

She reaches out for a welcoming can of soda. It pops open with a gush of fizz, having been disturbed by every rolling bump along the way.

She offers the open Dr. Papper to Sonetto.

**Sonetto**：Th-Thank you, but, uh ...

A bright yellow bag of snacks, puffed up with air, follows right after.

**Regulus**：Come on, mate, relax a little. Those poor frazzled nerves need a rest, especially after dealing with those slimy, screeching Scyllas.

**APPLe**：You've raised an excellent point, Captain!

**Regulus**：Too right I have, Mr. APPLe. Always nice to have my brilliance recognized. Uh, only, what do you mean?

**APPLe**：Yes, one of the many trials he faced after the Trojan War.

**APPLe**：There was also the Cyclops Polyphemus, the wind ruler Aeolus, the master sorceress Circe ... Each added to his misery.

**APPLe**：And yet, Odysseus never abandoned his quest to return home to Ithaca.

**Regulus**：Sounds to me like getting back there was a lot more trouble than it was worth.

**APPLe**：Not everyone is as suited to a life on the open seas as you are, Captain.

**APPLe**：For Odysseus, it was never a question of whether facing those trials was logical. He was driven by his "telos," or purpose. He had no choice but to follow it.

**Sonetto**：So, he would have endured anything to reach his home again.

**APPLe**：Precisely. We each have a purpose shaped by our beliefs and desires. For Odysseus, it was returning to his family.

**Regulus**：Fair play to him, I guess. But you wouldn't catch me dodging sea monsters just for a homecoming party.

**APPLe**：In ancient myth, hardship is almost a prerequisite for anyone seeking true knowledge, heroism, or reward.

**Regulus**：But didn't he lose all his crewmates along the way? I can't even imagine how he survived all that by himself.

**APPLe**：It was a considerable loss, Captain. Some might argue too great for the reward. Though one could say that was precisely why Odysseus went to the lengths he did.

**Vertin**：I suppose it depends on what you value most.

**Vertin**：Against all the pain and suffering he went through, all the friends that he lost, it's hard not to question if returning home was worth it.

**Regulus**：Putting aside all the grim and nasty bits, you've got to admit he had one hell of a journey. Staring down a Cyclops, facing off with the Scylla, surviving a Siren's song ...

**Regulus**：Scyllas, Sirens, ah, what's the other one again? Right, Nymphs. Though, I'd say they're the weakest of the three.

She offers her assessment with the kind of confidence earned by having never encountered any of them.

Amid the soft churn of waves, one begins to stir out of sync, as if agitated by their conversation.

**Sonetto**：I'm not sure most arcanobiologists would agree with you. At least, not from a taxonomic standpoint.

**Regulus**：I don't give a toss about that. I'm talking battle strategy. If all three of them come at me at once, which do I do in first? The weakest one, or the one most likely to gobble me up?

**Vertin**：Well, Regulus? Would you prioritize the Nymph?

Only for a sudden weight to pull the one in front down toward the sea.

**Narcissus**：Come along, heroes, follow me!

**Vertin**：Ms. Narcissus?

**Narcissus**：No need to worry. Just hold your breath.

Sonetto stumbles forward and slips from the rail.

Another hand catches her just in time.

**Regulus**：Hang on a second. This doesn't feel right. She's trying to ...

Regulus's voice comes out taut.

**Regulus**：... drag us into the sea!

**APPLe**：Careful, Captain!

The hidden island is finally revealed.

**Regulus**：Hah! Wakey wakey, sleepyheads. Nice to have you back.

**Sonetto**：What happened?

**Regulus**：I'll spare you the blow-by-blow. Good news first: we weren't torn apart by sea monsters, and we aren't in the belly of a whale either.

The plaza is overflowing with people. Waves crash against the rocks along the shore, white spray nearly splashing onto their hair and sleeves.

The "uninvited guests" find themselves bound to a pillar at the edge of the plaza, powerless to do anything but watch.

*thud*

A figure steps onto the altar at the center.

**???**：Citizens of Ephaistonos, hear me now.

**Sonetto**：...!

**???**：But I beseech you all: pay their transgression no mind! For in the fullness of our sacred ceremony, they will be dealt with appropriately.

Her voice is unconcerned, but her eyes hit them like something solid, freezing body and mind together.

**???**：And with that, let us return to our purpose here. For the beast stirs within its lair once more, and when its long slumber is broken, its terrible wrath will be levied upon us again.

**Hedone**：Which is why I, Hedone, with the memories of my past life reclaimed, will cast our enemy back into the depths of Tartarus, leading us once again to victory!

**Islander I**：All hail Lady Hedone!

**Islander II**：Victory belongs to Lady Hedone!

The crowd cheers, raising their golden cups high. Drops of purple-red wine fly through the air, creating a hazy, heady scent.

**Hedone**：Apollo!

**Apollo**：Lady Hedone, my divination awaits your command.

**Hedone**：Ahahahaha! I recall when we last stood here. An epoch come and gone, and yet it is as it was ever thus.
