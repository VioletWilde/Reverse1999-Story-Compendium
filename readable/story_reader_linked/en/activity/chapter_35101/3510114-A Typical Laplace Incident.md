**Ludwig**: He asked me to join the Staff Committee and oppose this policy reform. I agreed.

**Enigma**: Oh, I see. Then if Laplace develops enough groundbreaking "Storm" technologies before the Foundation does, we can turn the tables on them.

**Ludwig**: Indeed, it would give us better leverage, if nothing else.

**Ludwig**: But we don't have much time on our hands.

**Ludwig**: Their Hawks are gaining support from more commissioners as we speak. For now, all I can do is delay the proposal's review process.

**Enigma**: Tch, typical politics.

**Enigma**: You once led the Plesetsk branch, Mr. Ludwig, and as I am sure you can see, I have never been one for matters of politics or leadership.

**Enigma**: I was only appointed to maximize the chance of preserving and continuing Director Lucy's research.

**Enigma**: That is to say, is there any chance we could bring Director Lucy back to Laplace?

**Ludwig**: Whether possible or not, our first step in either case will be to stop this policy reform.

**Ludwig**: If Ms. Latham's investigation yields no significant progress soon, all we can do is wait for good news from Lorentz Butterfly.

**Ludwig**: I'm confident she'll bring us something. She's always been one step ahead.

**Enigma**: You sound quite certain of her. But we know nothing about this Lorentz Butterfly aside from a few unorthodox papers and provocative letters.

The bird-headed scientist chooses to remain silent, but the cryptic response proves a poor choice when dealing with an expert cryptologist.

**Enigma**: Wait a minute. Impossible!

**Enigma**: You know who Lorentz Butterfly is, don't you?

**Ludwig**: Hmm. It was the year of 1999. At the time, I was a visiting professor at Cambridge University. There was a student in my class, far too brilliant for her exceptionally young age.

**Ludwig**: The staff told me she wasn't an enrolled student. She simply came every week to attend my lecture.

**Ludwig**: I had intended to recommend her for admission, but, of course, the "Storm" disrupted everything.

**Ludwig**: I ended up leaving Cambridge and joining Laplace myself.

**Ludwig**: After the second "Storm," I paid the university a visit and saw that young lady again.

**Enigma**: Are you saying she survived multiple "Storms" on her own?

**Ludwig**: Yes. Though she didn't share the details, I could tell she was hiding a secret, or running from some serious danger.

**Ludwig**: She had an exceptional gift for the sciences. Naturally, I invited her to join Laplace.

**Enigma**: But from what little we know of her, she's ... dangerous.
